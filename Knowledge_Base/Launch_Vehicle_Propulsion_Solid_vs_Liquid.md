# Launch Vehicle Propulsion (Solid vs Liquid)

## Overview

Launch vehicle propulsion systems are classified into two primary categories: solid and liquid. Each type has its unique advantages and disadvantages, which are discussed in this entry.

### Solid Propulsion

Solid propulsion systems use a non-reusable, solid fuel and oxidizer combination. The fuel and oxidizer are cast together in a single unit, which is then ignited to produce thrust.

- **Advantages:**
  - Simple design and low cost
  - High thrust-to-weight ratio
  - No need for complex fuel handling systems
- **Disadvantages:**
  - Limited control over thrust and specific impulse
  - Difficult to restart or shut down
  - Limited reusability

### Liquid Propulsion

Liquid propulsion systems use a reusable, liquid fuel and oxidizer combination. The fuel and oxidizer are stored in separate tanks and are pumped into a combustion chamber to produce thrust.

- **Advantages:**
  - High specific impulse and efficient combustion
  - Ability to control thrust and specific impulse
  - Reusability and flexibility
- **Disadvantages:**
  - Complex design and high cost
  - Requires sophisticated fuel handling systems
  - Higher risk of leaks and contamination

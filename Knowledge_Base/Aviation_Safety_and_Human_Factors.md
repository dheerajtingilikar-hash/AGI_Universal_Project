# Aviation Safety and Human Factors

## Overview

Aviation safety and human factors are critical components of the aviation industry, focusing on the interaction between humans and technology to prevent accidents and ensure safe flight operations. The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) defines human factors as "the study of how humans interact with machines, systems, and environments."

### Definition

- **Human Factors**: The study of how humans interact with machines, systems, and environments. [[Human Factors]]
- **Aviation Safety**: The measures taken to prevent accidents and ensure safe flight operations. [[Aviation Safety]]

### Key Concepts

- **Crew Resource Management (CRM)**: A training program designed to improve communication, decision-making, and teamwork among flight crews. [[Crew Resource Management]]
- **Error Management**: A strategy for identifying and mitigating errors in flight operations. [[Error Management]]
- **Fatigue Management**: A system for monitoring and managing pilot fatigue to prevent accidents. [[Fatigue Management]]
- **Risk Management**: A process for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks in flight operations. [[Risk Management]]

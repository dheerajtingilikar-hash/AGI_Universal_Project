# Archaeology
Archaeology is the scientific study of [[Past Human Cultures]] through the recovery, analysis, and interpretation of [[Material Culture]].

## Overview
Archaeology involves the systematic excavation and study of [[Archaeological Sites]], including the analysis of [[Artifacts]], [[Architecture]], and [[Biofacts]]. The goal of archaeology is to understand the [[Behavior]], [[Technology]], and [[Social Organization]] of past human societies. Archaeologists use a variety of techniques, including [[Stratigraphy]], [[Typology]], and [[Radiocarbon Dating]], to reconstruct the past.

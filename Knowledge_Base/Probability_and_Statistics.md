# Probability and Statistics
Probability and Statistics is a branch of [[Mathematics]] that deals with the study of chance events and their outcomes. It involves the collection, analysis, interpretation, presentation, and organization of [[Data]].

## Overview
[[Probability Theory]] is a fundamental concept in Probability and Statistics, which assigns a numerical value to the likelihood of an event occurring. This value, known as the [[Probability]] of the event, is always between 0 and 1. [[Statistics]], on the other hand, is the study of the collection, analysis, interpretation, presentation, and organization of data. It involves the use of [[Statistical Methods]] to extract insights and meaning from data.

## Concept Map
The key concepts in Probability and Statistics can be organized into the following categories:
* [[Descriptive Statistics]]: deals with the summary and description of data, including measures of central tendency and variability.
* [[Inferential Statistics]]: involves making conclusions or predictions about a population based on a sample of data, using techniques such as [[Hypothesis Testing]] and [[Confidence Intervals]].
* [[Probability Distributions]]: includes the study of different types of distributions, such as the [[Normal Distribution]] and the [[Binomial Distribution]].
* [[Statistical Inference]]: involves the use of statistical methods to make inferences about a population, including [[Estimation]] and [[Prediction]]. 

Related topics include: [[Machine Learning]], [[Data Mining]], and [[Data Science]].

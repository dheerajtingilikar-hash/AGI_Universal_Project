# Stellar Nucleosynthesis
Stellar nucleosynthesis is the process by which [[stars]] create heavier [[element]]s from lighter ones through [[nuclear reaction]]s.

## Overview
Stellar nucleosynthesis occurs in the [[core]] of stars, where [[temperature]]s and [[pressure]]s are high enough to sustain [[nuclear fusion]] reactions. These reactions involve the combination of [[atomic nucleus|atomic nuclei]] to form heavier nuclei, releasing [[energy]] in the process. The most common nuclear fusion reaction in stars is the [[proton-proton chain reaction]], which produces [[helium]] from [[hydrogen]].

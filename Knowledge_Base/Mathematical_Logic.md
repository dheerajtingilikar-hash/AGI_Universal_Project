# Mathematical Logic
Mathematical logic is a subfield of [[Mathematics]] that explores the principles of [[Logical Reasoning]] and [[Formal Language]].

## Overview
Mathematical logic is concerned with the study of [[Logical Operators]], [[Propositional Logic]], and [[Predicate Logic]]. It provides a foundation for the development of [[Formal Systems]] and [[Model Theory]]. Key areas of mathematical logic include [[Proof Theory]], [[Model Theory]], and [[Set Theory]].

## Concept Map
* [[Propositional Logic]]: studies [[Logical Operators]] and [[Truth Tables]]
* [[Predicate Logic]]: extends propositional logic to include [[Quantifiers]] and [[Predicates]]
* [[Model Theory]]: examines the relationship between [[Formal Theories]] and their [[Models]]
* [[Proof Theory]]: investigates the structure of [[Formal Proofs]] and [[Deductive Reasoning]]
* [[Set Theory]]: provides a foundation for mathematics using [[Sets]] and [[Functions]]

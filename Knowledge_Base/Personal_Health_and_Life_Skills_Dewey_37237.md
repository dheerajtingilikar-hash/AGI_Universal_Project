# Personal Health and Life Skills (Dewey 372.37)

## Overview

Personal health and life skills refer to the knowledge, attitudes, and practices necessary for individuals to maintain their physical, mental, and emotional well-being throughout their lives. This concept encompasses a broad range of skills, including self-care, stress management, nutrition, physical activity, and health literacy.

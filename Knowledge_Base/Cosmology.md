# Cosmology
Cosmology is the study of the origin, evolution, and fate of the [[Universe]]. It is a multidisciplinary field that combines [[Astronomy]], [[Astrophysics]], and [[Cosmogony]] to understand the nature of the cosmos.

## Overview
Cosmology seeks to answer fundamental questions about the [[Universe]], such as its age, size, and composition. It also explores the formation and evolution of [[Galaxies]], [[Stars]], and [[Planets]]. The field of cosmology is closely related to [[Particle Physics]] and [[Quantum Mechanics]], as it relies on the understanding of the behavior of [[Matter]] and [[Energy]] at the smallest scales.

## Concept Map
The study of cosmology involves several key concepts, including:
* [[Big Bang Theory]]: the leading explanation for the origin and evolution of the universe
* [[Dark Matter]]: a type of matter that does not emit or reflect [[Light]] and is thought to make up approximately 27% of the universe's mass-energy density
* [[Dark Energy]]: a mysterious component that drives the accelerating expansion of the universe
* [[Inflation Theory]]: a theoretical framework that describes the rapid expansion of the universe in the early stages of its evolution
* [[Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation]]: the residual heat from the initial explosion of the Big Bang, which is thought to be a key evidence for the Big Bang Theory
* [[Large-scale Structure of the Universe]]: the distribution of [[Galaxies]] and [[Galaxy Clusters]] on large scales, which is influenced by the properties of dark matter and dark energy.

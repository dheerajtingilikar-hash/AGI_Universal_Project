# Sociology
Sociology is the scientific study of [[Society]], including patterns of [[Social Relationship|social relationships]], [[Social Interaction|social interactions]], and [[Culture|cultures]] that shape human behavior.

## Overview
Sociology examines the development, structure, and functioning of [[Human Society|human societies]], from small groups to large-scale institutions. It involves the study of [[Social Institution|social institutions]], such as [[Family]], [[Education]], [[Religion]], and [[Economy|economies]], as well as the relationships between individuals, groups, and societies. Sociologists use a range of [[Research Method|research methods]], including [[Survey Research|surveys]], [[Experiment|experiments]], and [[Ethnography|ethnographies]], to investigate [[Social Phenomenon|social phenomena]].

## Concept Map
The field of sociology encompasses various subfields, including:
* [[Social Stratification|Social stratification]]: the study of [[Social Inequality|social inequality]] and [[Social Mobility|social mobility]]
* [[Criminology|Criminology]]: the study of [[Crime|crime]] and [[Deviance|deviance]]
* [[Demography|Demography]]: the study of [[Population|population]] dynamics and [[Demographic Transition|demographic transition]]
* [[Urban Sociology|Urban sociology]]: the study of [[City|cities]] and [[Urbanization|urbanization]]
* [[Rural Sociology|Rural sociology]]: the study of [[Rural Area|rural areas]] and [[Agriculture|agriculture]]
* [[Sociology of Education|Sociology of education]]: the study of [[Education|education]] and its relationship to [[Society|society]]

Related fields include:

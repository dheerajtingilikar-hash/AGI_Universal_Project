# Anti-Satellite Weapons (ASAT)

## Overview

Anti-satellite weapons (ASAT) are designed to destroy or disable satellites in orbit around the Earth. These weapons can be launched from the ground, air, or space, and can use various methods to target and destroy satellites, including kinetic energy interceptors, directed energy, and explosive warheads.

### Types of ASAT Systems

- **Kinetic Energy ASATs**: Use a physical object, such as a missile or a projectile, to collide with and destroy a satellite.
- **Directed Energy ASATs**: Use high-powered lasers or other forms of directed energy to damage or destroy a satellite.
- **Explosive ASATs**: Use a warhead to detonate near a satellite, causing damage or destruction.

### History of ASAT Development

- **Soviet Union**: Conducted the first ASAT test in 1964, using a kinetic energy interceptor.
- **United States**: Developed the ASAT program in the 1980s, but it was canceled in 1988 due to international pressure.
- **China**: Conducted an ASAT test in 2007, using a kinetic energy interceptor.
- **India**: Conducted an ASAT test in 2019, using a kinetic energy interceptor.

# Biotechnology
Biotechnology is the use of [[Biology]] and [[Technology]] to develop new products, technologies, and [[Therapy|therapeutic]] approaches.

## Overview
Biotechnology involves the manipulation of [[Genetics|genetic]] material, [[Protein|proteins]], and other [[Biomolecule|biomolecules]] to achieve specific goals, such as the production of [[Pharmaceuticals|pharmaceuticals]], [[Vaccine|vaccines]], and [[Diagnostic|diagnostics]]. It has applications in various fields, including [[Medicine]], [[Agriculture]], and [[Environmental Science]].

## Concept Map
The concept of biotechnology can be broken down into several key areas:
* [[Genetic Engineering]]: the direct manipulation of an organism's [[Genome|genome]]
* [[Gene Expression]]: the process by which the information encoded in a [[Gene|gene]] is converted into a functional product
* [[Protein Engineering]]: the design and construction of new [[Protein|proteins]] with specific functions
* [[Synthetic Biology]]: the design and construction of new [[Biological System|biological systems]] or the redesign of existing ones
* [[Bioinformatics]]: the use of [[Computer Science]] and [[Mathematics]] to analyze and interpret [[Biological Data|biological data]]

## Applications
Biotechnology has a wide range of applications, including:
* [[Medical Biotechnology]]: the use of biotechnology to develop new [[Therapy|therapies]] and [[Diagnostic|diagnostics]] for human diseases
* [[Agricultural Biotechnology]]: the use of biotechnology to improve crop yields and develop new [[Food|food]] products
* [[Environmental Biotechnology]]: the use of biotechnology to develop new approaches to [[Environmental Conservation|environmental conservation]] and [[Sustainability|sustainability]]

## Related Fields
Biotechnology is closely related to other fields, including:
* [[Molecular Biology]]: the study of the structure and function of [[Biomolecule|biomolecules]]
* [[Cell Biology]]: the study of the structure and function of [[Cell|cells]]
* [[Biochemistry]]: the study of the chemical processes that occur within living organisms

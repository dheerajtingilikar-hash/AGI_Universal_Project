# Telecommunications
Telecommunications refers to the transmission and reception of information over long distances through various electronic means, including [[Telephone]] networks, [[Internet]] protocols, and [[Satellite Communications]].

## Overview
Telecommunications is a critical component of modern society, enabling global communication, information exchange, and economic development. The field encompasses a broad range of technologies, including [[Wireless Communication]], [[Fiber Optic Communication]], and [[Cable Television]].

## Concept Map
The concept of telecommunications can be broken down into several key areas, including:
* [[Network Architecture]]: The design and organization of telecommunications networks, including [[Local Area Network|LANs]], [[Wide Area Network|WANs]], and [[Metropolitan Area Network|MANs]].
* [[Signal Processing]]: The manipulation and transmission of signals over telecommunications networks, including [[Modulation]], [[Demodulation]], and [[Error Correction]].
* [[Communication Protocols]]: The standardized rules and procedures governing data communication over telecommunications networks, including [[TCP/IP]] and [[HTTP]].
* [[Telecommunication Services]]: The various services provided over telecommunications networks, including [[Voice Over Internet Protocol|VoIP]], [[Video Conferencing]], and [[Data Transmission]].

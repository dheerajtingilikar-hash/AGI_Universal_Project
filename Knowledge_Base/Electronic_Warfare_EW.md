# Electronic Warfare (EW)

## Overview

Electronic Warfare (EW) is a military tactic that involves the use of electromagnetic energy to disrupt or destroy an enemy's ability to use their electronic systems. EW is a critical component of modern warfare, as it can be used to gain a strategic advantage on the battlefield.

### Definition

EW is defined as the use of electromagnetic energy to attack or defend against an enemy's electronic systems. This can include a wide range of systems, including radar, communication systems, navigation systems, and electronic countermeasures (ECMs).

### Types of EW

There are several types of EW, including:

- **Electronic Attack (EA)**: The use of electromagnetic energy to attack or destroy an enemy's electronic systems.
- **Electronic Protection (EP)**: The use of electromagnetic energy to protect one's own electronic systems from enemy attack.
- **Electronic Support (ES)**: The use of electromagnetic energy to detect and identify an enemy's electronic systems.
- **Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)**: The use of electromagnetic energy to gather information about an enemy's electronic systems.

# Astrophysics
Astrophysics is the branch of [[Astronomy]] that deals with the physical nature of [[Celestial Objects]] and phenomena in the [[Universe]].

## Overview
Astrophysics involves the study of the [[Physics]] of the [[Universe]], including the behavior of [[Stars]], [[Galaxies]], and other [[Cosmic]] entities. It encompasses various subfields, such as [[Stellar Astrophysics]], [[Galactic Astrophysics]], and [[Cosmology]]. Astrophysicists use a combination of [[Observational Astronomy]] and [[Theoretical Physics]] to understand the workings of the [[Universe]].

## Concept Map
* [[Astrophysics]] 
	+ [[Stellar Astrophysics]]: study of [[Stars]] and their properties
	+ [[Galactic Astrophysics]]: study of [[Galaxies]] and their evolution
	+ [[Cosmology]]: study of the origin and evolution of the [[Universe]]
	+ [[Planetary Science]]: study of [[Planets]] and other [[Celestial Bodies]]
	+ [[Astrobiology]]: study of the origin and distribution of [[Life]] in the [[Universe]]

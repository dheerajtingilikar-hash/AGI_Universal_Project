# Decipherment of Extinct Scripts: Sumerian and Ancient Egyptian

## Overview

The decipherment of extinct scripts refers to the process of understanding and interpreting written languages that have been lost over time. This process has been crucial in the field of archaeology, allowing researchers to unlock the secrets of ancient civilizations. Two notable examples of extinct scripts are the Sumerian and Ancient Egyptian scripts.

### Sumerian Script

- **[[Cuneiform]]**: The Sumerian script, also known as cuneiform, was a complex writing system used by the ancient Sumerians in Mesopotamia (modern-day Iraq).
- **[[Hiero-Glyphic]]**: The decipherment of cuneiform was facilitated by the discovery of the Rosetta Stone, which featured the same text in three languages: ancient Greek, demotic script, and hieroglyphics.
- **[[Assyriologists]]**: Scholars such as George Smith and Edward Hincks played a crucial role in deciphering the cuneiform script.

### Ancient Egyptian Script

- **[[Hieroglyphics]]**: The Ancient Egyptian script, also known as hieroglyphics, was a complex writing system used by the ancient Egyptians.
- **[[Demotic Script]]**: The decipherment of hieroglyphics was also facilitated by the discovery of the Rosetta Stone.
- **[[Egyptologists]]**: Scholars such as Jean-François Champollion and Thomas Young played a crucial role in deciphering the hieroglyphic script.

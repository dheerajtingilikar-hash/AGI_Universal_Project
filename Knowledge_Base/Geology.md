# Geology
Geology is the scientific study of the [[Earth]]'s physical structure, composition, and processes. It encompasses the study of [[Rocks]], [[Minerals]], and [[Landforms]], as well as the processes that shape the [[Planet]].

## Overview
Geology is an interdisciplinary field that draws on [[Physics]], [[Chemistry]], and [[Biology]] to understand the [[Earth]]'s systems and processes. It is divided into several subfields, including [[Physical Geology]], [[Historical Geology]], and [[Economic Geology]]. Geologists use a range of techniques, including [[Geophysical Surveying]] and [[Geochemical Analysis]], to study the [[Earth]]'s composition and processes.

## Concept Map
* [[Geology]] 
	+ [[Physical Geology]]: study of the [[Earth]]'s physical structure and processes
	+ [[Historical Geology]]: study of the [[Earth]]'s history and evolution
	+ [[Economic Geology]]: study of the [[Earth]]'s resources and economic significance
* [[Rocks]] 
	+ [[Igneous Rocks]]: formed from [[Magma]] or [[Lava]]
	+ [[Sedimentary Rocks]]: formed from [[Sediment]] or [[Mineral]] particles
	+ [[Metamorphic Rocks]]: formed from [[Metamorphism]] of existing rocks
* [[Minerals]] 
	+ [[Native Elements]]: naturally occurring [[Elements]]
	+ [[Mineral Compounds]]: combinations of [[Elements]] in a specific ratio
* [[Landforms]] 
	+ [[Mountains]]: formed through [[Tectonic]] processes
	+ [[Valleys]]: formed through [[Erosion]] and [[Deposition]] processes
	+ [[Plains]]: formed through [[Weathering]] and [[Erosion]] processes

## Related Fields
* [[Geophysics]]: study of the [[Earth]]'s physical properties and processes
* [[Geochemistry]]: study of the [[Earth]]'s chemical composition and processes
* [[Paleontology]]: study of [[Fossils]] and the [[Earth]]'s history of life

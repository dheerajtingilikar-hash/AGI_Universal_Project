# Quantum Biology
Quantum biology is an interdisciplinary field that applies the principles of [[Quantum Mechanics]] to study the behavior of [[Molecules]] and [[Cells]] in living organisms.

## Overview
Quantum biology explores the role of quantum phenomena, such as [[Superposition]], [[Entanglement]], and [[Tunneling]], in biological processes. These phenomena have been observed in various biological systems, including [[Photosynthesis]], [[Enzyme Catalysis]], and [[DNA Mutations]]. The field aims to understand how quantum effects influence the behavior of biological molecules and their interactions, and to develop new theories and models to describe these phenomena.

## Concept Map
The concept map of quantum biology includes the following key topics:
* [[Quantum Mechanics]]: the theoretical framework for understanding quantum phenomena
* [[Biophysics]]: the study of the physical principles underlying biological processes
* [[Molecular Biology]]: the study of the structure and function of biological molecules
* [[Quantum Computing]]: the use of quantum-mechanical phenomena for computational purposes
* [[Biological Systems]]: the study of complex biological systems, including [[Ecological Systems]] and [[Neurological Systems]]

Key concepts in quantum biology include:
* [[Quantum Coherence]]: the ability of quantum systems to exist in multiple states simultaneously
* [[Quantum Decoherence]]: the loss of quantum coherence due to interactions with the environment
* [[Quantum Entanglement]]: the phenomenon of quantum systems becoming connected and correlated
* [[Quantum Tunneling]]: the ability of quantum systems to pass through energy barriers

Related fields include:
* [[Chemical Physics]]: the study of the physical principles underlying chemical reactions
* [[Biological Physics]]: the study of the physical principles underlying biological processes
* [[Nanotechnology]]: the study and application of materials and devices on the nanoscale

Key researchers in the field of quantum biology include:
* [[Erwin Schrödinger]]: a physicist who proposed the concept of quantum biology
* [[Francis Crick]]: a biologist who applied quantum mechanics to the study of DNA
* [[Roger Penrose]]: a physicist and mathematician who has worked on the application of quantum mechanics to consciousness and biology.

# Epistemology
Epistemology is the branch of [[Philosophy]] that deals with the nature, sources, and limits of [[Knowledge]].

## Overview
Epistemology is concerned with understanding how we acquire [[Belief]]s, how we justify them, and what constitutes [[Truth]]. It involves the study of the relationship between the [[Mind]] and the [[World]], and the ways in which we can gain [[Understanding]] of reality. Epistemology is a fundamental area of inquiry in [[Philosophy]], and its concepts and theories have far-reaching implications for many fields, including [[Science]], [[Ethics]], and [[Metaphysics]].

## Concept Map
The following concepts are central to epistemology:
* [[Justification]]: the process of providing reasons or evidence to support a [[Belief]]
* [[Truth]]: the state of being in accordance with [[Reality]]
* [[Knowledge]]: [[Belief]] that is both [[True]] and [[Justified]]
* [[Skepticism]]: the view that we can never be certain of any [[Belief]]
* [[Rationalism]]: the view that [[Knowledge]] can be acquired through [[Reason]] alone
* [[Empiricism]]: the view that [[Knowledge]] can be acquired through [[Experience]] and [[Observation]]
* [[A Priori]]: [[Knowledge]] that is independent of [[Experience]]
* [[A Posteriori]]: [[Knowledge]] that is dependent on [[Experience]]

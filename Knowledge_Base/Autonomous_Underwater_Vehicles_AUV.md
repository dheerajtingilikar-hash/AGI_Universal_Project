# Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUV)

## Overview

An Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) is a type of underwater robot that operates independently, without human intervention, to collect and transmit data from the ocean floor. AUVs are designed to perform a variety of tasks, including oceanographic research, offshore oil and gas exploration, and underwater inspection and maintenance.

### Characteristics

- **Autonomy**: AUVs operate independently, using pre-programmed instructions and sensors to navigate and collect data.
- **Depth**: AUVs can operate at depths ranging from a few meters to several thousand meters.
- **Speed**: AUVs can reach speeds of up to 10 knots (18.5 km/h).
- **Endurance**: AUVs can operate for several hours or days, depending on their power source and design.

### Applications

- **Oceanographic Research**: AUVs are used to study ocean currents, temperature, and chemistry.
- **Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration**: AUVs are used to inspect and maintain offshore oil and gas platforms.
- **Underwater Inspection and Maintenance**: AUVs are used to inspect and maintain underwater infrastructure, such as pipelines and bridges.

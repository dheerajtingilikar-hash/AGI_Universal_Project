# Pharmacology: Psychotropic Drug Classes and Mechanism of Action

## Overview

Psychotropic drugs are a class of medications that affect the central nervous system (CNS) and are used to treat various psychiatric and neurological disorders. These drugs can be broadly classified into several categories based on their mechanism of action, receptor targets, and therapeutic effects.

## Concept Map

### I. Psychostimulants

- **Mechanism of Action:** Increase the release of neurotransmitters such as dopamine and norepinephrine, and block the reuptake of these neurotransmitters.
- **Examples:** [[Methylphenidate]], [[Amphetamine]], [[Modafinil]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Improve attention, concentration, and impulse control.

### II. Antipsychotics

- **Mechanism of Action:** Block dopamine receptors, particularly D2 receptors, in the mesolimbic pathway.
- **Examples:** [[Haloperidol]], [[Risperidone]], [[Olanzapine]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and other psychotic disorders.

### III. Antidepressants

- **Mechanism of Action:** Increase the release of neurotransmitters such as serotonin and norepinephrine, and block the reuptake of these neurotransmitters.
- **Examples:** [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors (SSRIs)]], [[Tricyclic Antidepressants (TCAs)]], [[Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat depression, anxiety disorders, and other mood disorders.

### IV. Anxiolytics

- **Mechanism of Action:** Enhance the activity of GABA, an inhibitory neurotransmitter, in the CNS.
- **Examples:** [[Benzodiazepines]], [[Barbiturates]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat anxiety disorders, insomnia, and seizures.

### V. Sedatives and Hypnotics

- **Mechanism of Action:** Enhance the activity of GABA, an inhibitory neurotransmitter, in the CNS.
- **Examples:** [[Benzodiazepines]], [[Barbiturates]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat insomnia, anxiety disorders, and seizures.

### VI. Mood Stabilizers

- **Mechanism of Action:** Modulate the activity of various neurotransmitters, including serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine.
- **Examples:** [[Lithium]], [[Valproate]], [[Carbamazepine]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat bipolar disorder, mood swings, and other mood disorders.

### VII. Anticonvulsants

- **Mechanism of Action:** Modulate the activity of various neurotransmitters, including GABA and glutamate.
- **Examples:** [[Phenytoin]], [[Carbamazepine]], [[Valproate]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat epilepsy, seizures, and other neurological disorders.

### VIII. Opioids

- **Mechanism of Action:** Bind to opioid receptors in the CNS, producing analgesia and euphoria.
- **Examples:** [[Morphine]], [[Fentanyl]], [[Heroin]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat pain, particularly chronic pain.

### IX. Cannabinoids

- **Mechanism of Action:** Bind to cannabinoid receptors in the CNS, producing analgesia, euphoria, and other effects.
- **Examples:** [[THC]], [[CBD]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat pain, nausea, and other symptoms associated with various medical conditions.

### X. Other Psychotropic Drugs

- **Mechanism of Action:** Vary depending on the specific drug and its therapeutic effects.
- **Examples:** [[Ketamine]], [[PCP]], [[MDMA]]
- **Therapeutic Effects:** Treat various psychiatric and neurological disorders, including depression, anxiety, and post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD).

# Oceanography
Oceanography is the scientific study of the [[Ocean]] and its properties, phenomena, and life forms. It is an interdisciplinary field that combines aspects of [[Biology]], [[Chemistry]], [[Geology]], and [[Physics]] to understand the complex interactions within the ocean.

## Overview
Oceanography encompasses various branches, including [[Physical Oceanography]], [[Chemical Oceanography]], [[Biological Oceanography]], and [[Geological Oceanography]]. It involves the study of [[Ocean Currents]], [[Tides]], [[Waves]], and [[Marine Life]], as well as the impact of human activities on the ocean environment. Oceanographers use a range of techniques, including [[Remote Sensing]], [[Acoustic Tomography]], and [[In Situ Measurements]], to collect data and conduct research.

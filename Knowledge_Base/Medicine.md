# Medicine
Medicine is the [[Science]] of maintaining and restoring [[Health]] through the study, diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of [[Disease]].

## Overview
Medicine is a vast and complex field that encompasses various [[Discipline]]s, including [[Anatomy]], [[Physiology]], [[Pharmacology]], and [[Epidemiology]]. The primary goal of medicine is to provide [[Patient]] care, improve [[Quality of Life]], and reduce [[Mortality]] rates. Medicine has evolved significantly over the centuries, with advances in [[Technology]], [[Research]], and [[Clinical Practice]].

## Concept Map
The field of medicine can be divided into several key areas, including:
* [[Preventive Medicine]]: focuses on preventing [[Disease]] through [[Vaccination]], [[Screening]], and [[Health Promotion]]
* [[Diagnostic Medicine]]: involves the use of [[Imaging]], [[Laboratory Tests]], and [[Physical Examination]] to diagnose [[Disease]]
* [[Therapeutic Medicine]]: encompasses the treatment of [[Disease]] using [[Pharmacotherapy]], [[Surgery]], and other interventions
* [[Alternative Medicine]]: includes [[Complementary Therapy]] and [[Traditional Medicine]] approaches
* [[Research Medicine]]: involves the study of [[Disease]] mechanisms, [[Treatment Outcomes]], and [[Healthcare Systems]]

# Ancient History
Ancient History refers to the study of [[Human Civilization]] from the emergence of [[Writing Systems]] to the fall of the [[Western Roman Empire]]. This period is characterized by the development of complex societies, [[Empires]], and the rise of major [[World Religions]].

## Overview
Ancient History encompasses a broad range of [[Geographical Regions]], including [[Ancient Egypt]], [[Mesopotamia]], [[Ancient Greece]], and [[Ancient Rome]]. The period is marked by significant advancements in [[Technology]], [[Philosophy]], and the [[Arts]]. The study of Ancient History provides valuable insights into the social, cultural, and political developments of ancient societies.

## Concept Map
The study of Ancient History can be organized into several key areas, including:
* [[Chronology]]: The study of the timeline of major events and periods in Ancient History
* [[Archaeology]]: The study of material remains and artifacts from ancient societies
* [[Historiography]]: The study of the methods and approaches used to write and interpret Ancient History
* [[Cultural Exchange]]: The study of the exchange of ideas, goods, and technologies between ancient societies
* [[Imperialism]]: The study of the expansion and maintenance of [[Empires]] in Ancient History

Key figures and events in Ancient History include:
* [[Socrates]] and the development of [[Western Philosophy]]
* The construction of the [[Great Pyramid of Giza]] in [[Ancient Egypt]]
* The conquests of [[Alexander the Great]] and the spread of [[Hellenistic Culture]]
* The rise and fall of the [[Roman Republic]] and the [[Roman Empire]]

# Pattern Making and Draping
## Overview
Pattern making and draping are fundamental techniques used in the [[Fashion Design]] process to create [[Garment]]s that fit the human body. [[Pattern Making]] involves creating a template or pattern from which to cut [[Fabric]], while [[Draping]] involves manipulating and shaping fabric directly on a [[Dress Form]] or [[Mannequin]] to create a three-dimensional design. These techniques are used to create a wide range of garments, from [[Haute Couture]] to [[Ready-to-Wear]] clothing.

## Concept Map
The pattern making and draping process involves several key concepts, including:
* [[Fabric]] selection and [[Textile]] properties
* [[Body Measurement]]s and [[Fit]]
* [[Pattern Drafting]] and [[Grading]]
* [[Draping]] techniques, including [[Tucking]] and [[Pleating]]
* [[Garment Construction]] methods, such as [[Seam]]ing and [[Finishing]]
* [[Fashion Illustration]] and [[Design]] principles

These concepts are interconnected and inform one another throughout the pattern making and draping process. By understanding and applying these concepts, designers can create garments that are both aesthetically pleasing and well-constructed.

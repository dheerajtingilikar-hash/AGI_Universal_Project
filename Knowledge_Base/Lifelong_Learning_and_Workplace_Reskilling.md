# Lifelong Learning and Workplace Reskilling

## Overview

Lifelong learning and workplace reskilling refer to the continuous process of acquiring new knowledge, skills, and competencies throughout an individual's life, particularly in response to changing job requirements and technological advancements. This concept is essential in today's rapidly evolving work environment, where adaptability and continuous learning are crucial for career success and employability.

### Definition

Lifelong learning is a concept that emphasizes the importance of ongoing education and skill development throughout an individual's life, rather than focusing solely on formal education during childhood and adolescence. Workplace reskilling, on the other hand, involves the process of acquiring new skills and knowledge to remain relevant in the workforce, often in response to technological changes or shifts in industry demand.

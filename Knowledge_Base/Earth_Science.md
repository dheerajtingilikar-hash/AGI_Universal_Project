# Earth Science
Earth Science encompasses the study of the [[Earth]] and its various components, including the [[Atmosphere]], [[Hydrosphere]], [[Lithosphere]], and [[Biosphere]].

## Overview
Earth Science is an interdisciplinary field that combines [[Geology]], [[Meteorology]], [[Oceanography]], and [[Astronomy]] to understand the Earth's systems, processes, and phenomena. It involves the study of the Earth's structure, composition, and evolution, as well as the impact of human activities on the environment.

## Concept Map
* [[Geology]]: study of the Earth's physical structure, composition, and processes
	+ [[Plate Tectonics]]: theory that describes the movement of the Earth's lithosphere
	+ [[Rocks]]: classification and formation of igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rocks
* [[Meteorology]]: study of the Earth's atmosphere and weather patterns
	+ [[Climate]]: long-term atmospheric conditions and their impact on the environment
	+ [[Weathering]]: breakdown of rocks and minerals through exposure to wind, water, and ice
* [[Oceanography]]: study of the Earth's oceans and their role in the Earth's systems
	+ [[Ocean Currents]]: movement of water in the oceans and their impact on climate and marine life
	+ [[Marine Geology]]: study of the Earth's oceanic crust and the processes that shape it
* [[Astronomy]]: study of the universe and the Earth's place within it
	+ [[Solar System]]: study of the planets, moons, and other objects that orbit the Sun
	+ [[Cosmology]]: study of the origin, evolution, and fate of the universe

## Related Fields
* [[Environmental Science]]: study of the impact of human activities on the environment
* [[Ecology]]: study of the relationships between living organisms and their environment
* [[Geophysics]]: study of the Earth's internal and external physical processes

## Key Concepts
* [[Erosion]]: wearing away of rocks and soil through exposure to wind, water, and ice
* [[Deposition]]: process of sediment settling and accumulating in a new location
* [[Earthquakes]]: sudden release of energy that causes the ground to shake
* [[Volcanoes]]: landforms that occur when magma from the Earth's interior rises to the surface

# General Aviation and Avionics

## Overview

General aviation refers to the use of aircraft for non-commercial purposes, such as personal transportation, recreation, and business flights. It encompasses a wide range of activities, including flight training, aerial surveying, and medical transport. Avionics, a term derived from aviation electronics, refers to the electronic systems used in aircraft to control and navigate the flight.

# Abstract Algebra
Abstract algebra is the study of algebraic structures, such as [[Group (mathematics)|groups]], [[Ring (mathematics)|rings]], and [[Field (mathematics)|fields]]. It provides a framework for analyzing and describing the properties and behavior of these structures.

## Overview
Abstract algebra is a branch of [[Mathematics]] that deals with the study of algebraic structures in a general and abstract way. It involves the use of [[Set theory]] and [[Logic]] to define and analyze these structures, and to study their properties and relationships. Abstract algebra has numerous applications in [[Computer Science]], [[Physics]], and [[Engineering]].

## Concept Map
The following concept map illustrates the relationships between the main topics in abstract algebra:
- [[Group (mathematics)|Groups]]: sets with a binary operation that satisfies certain properties, such as [[Closure (mathematics)|closure]] and [[Associativity]]
- [[Ring (mathematics)|Rings]]: sets with two binary operations that satisfy certain properties, such as [[Distributivity]]
- [[Field (mathematics)|Fields]]: sets with two binary operations that satisfy certain properties, such as [[Commutativity]] and [[Existence of multiplicative inverse]]
- [[Homomorphism]]: a structure-preserving map between two algebraic structures
- [[Isomorphism]]: a bijective homomorphism between two algebraic structures
- [[Galois theory]]: the study of the symmetry of algebraic equations using abstract algebraic structures

## Key Results
- The [[Fundamental theorem of Galois theory]] establishes a connection between the [[Galois group]] of a [[Field extension]] and the [[Lattice (order theory)|lattice]] of intermediate fields.
- The [[Jordan-Holder theorem]] describes the structure of a [[Finite group]] in terms of its [[Composition series]].

## Applications
- [[Computer Science]]: abstract algebra is used in [[Cryptography]], [[Error-correcting code|error-correcting codes]], and [[Computer network|computer networks]]
- [[Physics]]: abstract algebra is used in [[Quantum mechanics]], [[Particle physics]], and [[Symmetry (physics)|symmetry]]
- [[Engineering]]: abstract algebra is used in [[Signal processing]], [[Control theory]], and [[Coding theory]]

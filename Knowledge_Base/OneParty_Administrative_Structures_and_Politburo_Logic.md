# One-Party Administrative Structures and Politburo Logic

## Overview

One-party administrative structures refer to systems of government where a single political party holds power and controls the state apparatus. This type of system is often associated with authoritarian or totalitarian regimes. The Politburo, a key component of one-party systems, is a small group of high-ranking officials who make key decisions and set policy.

### Definition

A one-party system is defined as a system of government where only one political party is allowed to exist or hold power.

### Characteristics

* **Authoritarianism**: One-party systems often exhibit authoritarian tendencies, with the ruling party exercising significant control over the population.
* **Centralization**: Power is typically concentrated in the hands of a small group of leaders, often the Politburo.
* **Suppression of opposition**: Opposition parties or dissenting voices are often suppressed or banned.
* **State control**: The state controls key sectors of the economy and society.

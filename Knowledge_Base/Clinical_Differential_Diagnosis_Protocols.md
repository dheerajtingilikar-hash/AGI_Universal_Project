# Clinical Differential Diagnosis Protocols

## Overview

Clinical differential diagnosis protocols are systematic approaches used by healthcare professionals to identify and rule out potential causes of a patient's symptoms. The primary goal of differential diagnosis is to arrive at a diagnosis that explains the patient's symptoms and guides treatment decisions.

### Definition

Differential diagnosis is the process of identifying multiple possible causes of a patient's symptoms and evaluating the likelihood of each cause.

### Key Principles

- **Thorough history taking**: Gathering detailed information about the patient's symptoms, medical history, and lifestyle.
- **Physical examination**: Conducting a comprehensive physical examination to identify potential signs and symptoms.
- **Laboratory and diagnostic testing**: Ordering relevant laboratory and diagnostic tests to support or rule out potential diagnoses.
- **Evidence-based medicine**: Using the best available evidence to inform diagnosis and treatment decisions.

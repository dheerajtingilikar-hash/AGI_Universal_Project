# Political Science
Political science is the [[Social Science]] that deals with the study of [[Government]], [[Politics]], and [[Policies]]. It involves the analysis of [[Political Systems]], [[Institutions]], and [[Behavior]].

## Overview
Political science is a broad field that encompasses various subfields, including [[Comparative Politics]], [[International Relations]], [[Political Theory]], and [[Public Policy]]. It uses a range of [[Methodologies]], including [[Qualitative Research]] and [[Quantitative Research]], to understand and explain [[Political Phenomena]].

## Concept Map
The concept map of political science includes:
* [[Government]]: the institution that exercises [[Authority]] over a [[State]]
* [[Politics]]: the process of [[Decision Making]] and [[Power]] distribution
* [[Policies]]: the plans and actions of [[Government]] to achieve [[Public Goals]]
* [[Political Systems]]: the structures and institutions that shape [[Politics]]
* [[Institutions]]: the [[Organizations]] and [[Norms]] that shape [[Political Behavior]]
* [[Behavior]]: the actions and interactions of [[Individuals]] and [[Groups]] in the [[Political Process]]

## Subfields
* [[Comparative Politics]]: the study of [[Political Systems]] across different [[Countries]]
* [[International Relations]]: the study of [[Relations]] between [[States]] and [[International Organizations]]
* [[Political Theory]]: the study of [[Ideas]] and [[Concepts]] that shape [[Politics]]
* [[Public Policy]]: the study of [[Policies]] and their [[Impact]] on [[Society]]

## Key Terms
* [[Democracy]]: a system of government where [[Power]] is held by the [[People]]
* [[Authoritarianism]]: a system of government where [[Power]] is held by a single [[Individual]] or [[Group]]
* [[Federalism]]: a system of government where [[Power]] is divided between a [[Central Authority]] and [[Subnational Units]]

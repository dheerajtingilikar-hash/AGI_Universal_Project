# Pastry and Patisserie
## Overview
Pastry and patisserie are terms used to describe the art of creating sweet and savory pastries, desserts, and other baked goods. [[Baking]] is a crucial aspect of pastry and patisserie, as it requires a deep understanding of [[Ingredients]], [[Techniques]], and [[Equipment]]. Pastry and patisserie have a rich history, dating back to ancient civilizations, and have evolved over time to include a wide range of [[Traditional Desserts]] and [[Modern Creations]].

## Concept Map
The concept of pastry and patisserie can be broken down into several key areas, including:
* [[Ingredients]]: [[Flour]], [[Sugar]], [[Eggs]], and [[Butter]] are essential components of pastry and patisserie
* [[Techniques]]: [[Laminating]], [[Folding]], and [[Baking]] are critical skills required for creating pastry and patisserie
* [[Equipment]]: [[Mixers]], [[Ovens]], and [[Molds]] are necessary tools for preparing and baking pastry and patisserie
* [[Traditional Desserts]]: [[Tarts]], [[Cakes]], and [[Pastries]] are classic examples of pastry and patisserie
* [[Modern Creations]]: [[Molecular Gastronomy]] and [[Innovative Ingredients]] have led to the development of new and unique pastry and patisserie creations
* [[Patisserie Shops]]: [[Bakeries]] and [[Cafes]] often specialize in selling pastry and patisserie, offering a wide range of sweet and savory treats to customers

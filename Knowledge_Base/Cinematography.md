# Cinematography
Cinematography refers to the art and technique of [[Camera]] operation and [[Film]] making, encompassing the [[Visual Arts]] and [[Techniques]] used to capture and convey a story or message through moving images.

## Overview
Cinematography involves the use of [[Camera Angles]], [[Lighting]], and [[Composition]] to create a visual aesthetic that enhances the narrative and emotional impact of a film. A [[Cinematographer]] is responsible for making key decisions regarding the visual elements of a film, including the selection of [[Camera Lenses]], [[Film Stock]], and [[Color Grading]]. The goal of cinematography is to create a cohesive and engaging visual style that supports the director's vision and enhances the overall storytelling.

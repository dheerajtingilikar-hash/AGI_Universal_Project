# International Data Privacy Standards (GDPR, NIS2, and PDPL 2026)

## Overview

The International Data Privacy Standards refer to a set of regulations and guidelines that govern the collection, storage, and processing of personal data across international borders. The three primary standards discussed in this entry are:

- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**: A regulation implemented by the European Union (EU) in 2018 to protect the personal data of EU citizens.
- **NIS2 Directive (NIS2)**: A directive implemented by the EU in 2021 to improve the security of networks and information systems.
- **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) 2026**: A law implemented by the Singapore government in 2026 to regulate the collection, storage, and processing of personal data.

## Key Terms

- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **Data Subject**: An individual whose personal data is being collected, stored, or processed.
- **Data Controller**: An organization or individual responsible for the collection, storage, and processing of personal data.
- **Data Processor**: An organization or individual responsible for processing personal data on behalf of a data controller.
- **Consent**: A freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of the data subject's wishes.

## References

- **GDPR**: Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data.
- **NIS2**: Directive (EU) 2021/1237 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2021 on measures for a high common level of cybersecurity across the Union.
- **PDPL 2026**: Personal Data Protection Law 2026 (Singapore).

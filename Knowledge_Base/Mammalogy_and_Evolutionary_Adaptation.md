# Mammalogy and Evolutionary Adaptation

## Overview

Mammalogy is the scientific study of mammals, encompassing their evolution, behavior, physiology, and systematics. Evolutionary adaptation is a fundamental concept in mammalogy, referring to the process by which mammals develop traits that enhance their survival and reproductive success in specific environments.

### Definition

- **Mammalogy**: The scientific study of mammals, including their classification, evolution, behavior, physiology, and systematics. [[Mammalogy]]
- **Evolutionary Adaptation**: The process by which organisms develop traits that enhance their survival and reproductive success in specific environments. [[Evolutionary Adaptation]]

### Key Concepts

- **Natural Selection**: The process by which individuals with favorable traits are more likely to survive and reproduce, passing those traits to their offspring. [[Natural Selection]]
- **Adaptation**: A trait that enhances an organism's survival and reproductive success in a specific environment. [[Adaptation]]
- **Phylogeny**: The study of the evolutionary history and relationships among organisms. [[Phylogeny]]
- **Speciation**: The process by which a new species emerges from an existing one. [[Speciation]]

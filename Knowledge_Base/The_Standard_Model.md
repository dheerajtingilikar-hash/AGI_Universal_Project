# The Standard Model
The Standard Model is a [[Theoretical Framework]] in [[Physics]] that describes the behavior of [[Fundamental Particles]] and their interactions.

## Overview
The Standard Model is a [[Quantum Field Theory]] that provides a detailed description of the strong, weak, and electromagnetic interactions. It is based on the concept of [[Symmetry]] and the idea that [[Fundamental Forces]] are mediated by [[Gauge Bosons]]. The Standard Model includes [[Quarks]], [[Leptons]], [[Higgs Boson]], and [[Gauge Bosons]] as its fundamental components.

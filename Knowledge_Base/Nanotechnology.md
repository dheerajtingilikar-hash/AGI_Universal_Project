# Nanotechnology
Nanotechnology refers to the manipulation and engineering of materials on a [[Nanoscale]], typically between 1 and 100 [[Nanometer|nanometers]] in size.

## Overview
Nanotechnology is a multidisciplinary field that combines principles from [[Physics]], [[Chemistry]], [[Biology]], and [[Engineering]] to design, synthesize, and apply nanoscale materials and systems. The unique properties of nanomaterials, such as their high [[Surface Area]] and [[Quantum Effect|quantum effects]], make them useful for a wide range of applications, including [[Medicine]], [[Energy]], and [[Electronics]].

## Concept Map
The field of nanotechnology can be divided into several key areas, including:
* [[Nanomaterials]]: the design and synthesis of materials with unique properties at the nanoscale
* [[Nanodevices]]: the creation of devices that utilize nanoscale materials and phenomena, such as [[Nanorobotics]] and [[Nanoelectromechanical Systems]]
* [[Nanofabrication]]: the processes used to create nanoscale structures and patterns, such as [[Lithography]] and [[Etching]]
* [[Nanocharacterization]]: the techniques used to study and analyze nanoscale materials and systems, such as [[Microscopy]] and [[Spectroscopy]]

# Orbital Debris Mitigation

## Overview

Orbital debris mitigation refers to the strategies and techniques employed to prevent, reduce, or remove human-made objects in Earth's orbit that pose a risk to operational spacecraft and satellites. The increasing number of satellites in orbit has led to a growing concern about the accumulation of debris, which can cause catastrophic collisions and lead to a cascade of further debris generation.

### Definition

Orbital debris mitigation is a critical aspect of space situational awareness and is essential for maintaining the long-term sustainability of space exploration and utilization.

### Scope

Orbital debris mitigation encompasses various activities, including:

* Designing and manufacturing satellites with debris mitigation in mind
* Implementing debris removal strategies
* Developing guidelines and regulations for responsible space operations
* Conducting research and development of new technologies for debris mitigation

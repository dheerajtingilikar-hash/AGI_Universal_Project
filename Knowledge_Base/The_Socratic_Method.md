# The Socratic Method
The Socratic Method is a form of [[Cooperative Learning]] that involves a dialogue between individuals, typically a teacher and student, to stimulate [[Critical Thinking]] and draw out ideas and underlying presuppositions.

## Overview
The Socratic Method is based on the [[Socratic Questioning]] technique, which involves asking a series of [[Open-Ended Questions]] to encourage critical thinking and to expose underlying assumptions. This method is used to encourage students to think for themselves and to develop their own understanding of a subject, rather than simply memorizing [[Facts]].

## Key Terms
- [[Socratic Irony]]: a technique used to expose underlying assumptions by pretending to be ignorant of the subject matter.
- [[Elenchos]]: a technique used to challenge and refute an opponent's argument.
- [[Maieutics]]: a technique used to help others give birth to their own ideas and understanding.

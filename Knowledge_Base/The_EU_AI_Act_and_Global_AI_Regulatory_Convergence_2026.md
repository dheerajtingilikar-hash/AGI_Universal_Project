# The EU AI Act and Global AI Regulatory Convergence 2026

## Overview

The EU AI Act, officially known as the Artificial Intelligence Act (AIA), is a proposed regulation by the European Union (EU) aimed at establishing a comprehensive framework for the development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence (AI) within the EU. The regulation is part of the EU's broader efforts to address the risks and challenges associated with AI, including bias, transparency, and accountability.

### Key Provisions

- **Risk-based approach**: The AIA proposes a risk-based approach to regulating AI, categorizing AI systems into different levels of risk based on their potential impact on society.
- **High-risk AI systems**: The regulation identifies high-risk AI systems, such as those used in healthcare, transportation, and finance, and imposes stricter requirements on their development and deployment.
- **Transparency and explainability**: The AIA requires AI systems to provide transparent and explainable decision-making processes, ensuring that users understand how AI-driven decisions are made.
- **Human oversight**: The regulation mandates human oversight and intervention in AI decision-making processes, particularly in high-risk applications.

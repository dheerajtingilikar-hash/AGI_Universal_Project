# Marine Biology
Marine biology is the scientific study of organisms that live in the ocean and other saltwater environments. It involves the examination of the behavior, physiology, and ecology of [[Marine Life|marine life]].

## Overview
Marine biology is an interdisciplinary field that combines concepts from [[Biology|biology]], [[Chemistry|chemistry]], [[Physics|physics]], and [[Geology|geology]] to understand the complex interactions between [[Marine Ecosystems|marine ecosystems]] and the organisms that inhabit them. Marine biologists study a wide range of topics, including [[Coral Reefs|coral reefs]], [[Ocean Currents|ocean currents]], and [[Marine Conservation|marine conservation]].

# Commercial Aircraft Engineering

## Overview

Commercial aircraft engineering is a multidisciplinary field that involves the design, development, testing, and maintenance of commercial aircraft. It encompasses various aspects, including aerodynamics, structures, propulsion systems, avionics, and systems integration.

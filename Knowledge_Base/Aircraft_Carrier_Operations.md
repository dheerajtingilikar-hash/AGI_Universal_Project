# Aircraft Carrier Operations

## Overview

Aircraft carrier operations refer to the procedures and protocols involved in the deployment, maintenance, and operation of aircraft carriers. These vessels serve as floating airbases, enabling naval forces to project airpower across vast distances.

### Definition

Aircraft carrier operations involve the integration of various systems, including:

- **[[Aircraft]]**: Fixed-wing or rotary-wing aircraft that operate from the carrier's deck.
- **[[Catapult]]**: A system used to launch aircraft from the carrier's deck.
- **[[Arresting Gear]]**: A system used to recover aircraft on the carrier's deck.
- **[[Flight Deck]]**: The deck of the aircraft carrier where aircraft take off and land.

### Key Concepts

- **[[Aircraft Carrier Class]]**: A classification system for aircraft carriers based on their size, capabilities, and design.
- **[[Carrier Air Wing]]**: A group of aircraft and support personnel that operate from a single aircraft carrier.
- **[[Deck Operations]]**: The procedures and protocols involved in the movement of aircraft, personnel, and equipment on the flight deck.

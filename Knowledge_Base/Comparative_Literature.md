# Comparative Literature
Comparative literature is an [[Interdisciplinary]] field of study that analyzes and compares [[Literary Works]] from different [[Culture]]s, [[Language]]s, and [[Historical Period]]s.

## Overview
Comparative literature examines the similarities and differences between [[Literary Movements]], [[Literary Genres]], and [[Literary Themes]] across various [[National Literature]]s. This field of study aims to understand the [[Cultural Context]] and [[Historical Context]] of literary works, as well as their [[Social Impact]] and [[Political Significance]]. Comparative literature also explores the [[Translation]] and [[Reception]] of literary works in different cultures.

# Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAV/Drones)

## Overview

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), commonly referred to as drones, are aircraft that operate without a human pilot on board. They are remotely controlled or can fly autonomously using onboard computers and sensors. UAVs have various applications in fields such as military, agriculture, construction, and consumer markets.

### History

The concept of UAVs dates back to the early 20th century, with the first recorded use of a UAV in 1916 by the British Royal Flying Corps. However, modern UAVs began to take shape in the 1980s with the development of the MQ-1 Predator, a remotely controlled aircraft used for reconnaissance and surveillance.

### Types

UAVs can be classified into several categories:

- **Recreational UAVs**: Small, consumer-grade drones used for hobby purposes.
- **Civil UAVs**: Used for commercial applications such as aerial photography, surveying, and inspection.
- **Military UAVs**: Used for reconnaissance, surveillance, and combat operations.
- **Research UAVs**: Used for scientific research and experimentation.

- **Autonomy**: The ability of the UAV to operate independently.

# Stochastic Processes
Stochastic processes are mathematical models used to describe the behavior of systems that are subject to random fluctuations. These processes are widely used in various fields, including [[Physics]], [[Engineering]], [[Economics]], and [[Biology]].

## Overview
Stochastic processes are characterized by their inherent randomness, which is often modeled using [[Probability Theory]]. They can be used to describe a wide range of phenomena, from the motion of particles in a [[Fluid]] to the behavior of [[Financial Markets]]. Stochastic processes can be classified into different types, including [[Markov Chains]], [[Random Walks]], and [[Brownian Motion]].

## Concept Map
The concept of stochastic processes is closely related to other mathematical and scientific concepts, including:
* [[Chaos Theory]]: the study of complex and dynamic systems that are highly sensitive to initial conditions
* [[Fractals]]: geometric shapes that exhibit self-similarity at different scales
* [[Information Theory]]: the study of the quantification, storage, and communication of information
* [[Machine Learning]]: a field of study that focuses on the development of algorithms and statistical models that enable machines to learn from data
* [[Signal Processing]]: the analysis, modification, and synthesis of signals, which are functions that convey information

## Key Concepts
* [[Stationarity]]: a property of stochastic processes that describes the invariance of the process under time shifts
* [[Ergodicity]]: a property of stochastic processes that describes the equivalence of time averages and ensemble averages
* [[Autocorrelation]]: a measure of the correlation between a stochastic process and its time-shifted version
* [[Power Spectral Density]]: a measure of the distribution of power in a stochastic process across different frequencies

## Applications
Stochastic processes have a wide range of applications in various fields, including:
* [[Finance]]: stochastic processes are used to model the behavior of financial markets and to price [[Derivatives]]
* [[Engineering]]: stochastic processes are used to model the behavior of complex systems, such as [[Communication Networks]] and [[Transportation Systems]]
* [[Biology]]: stochastic processes are used to model the behavior of biological systems, such as [[Population Dynamics]] and [[Epidemiology]]

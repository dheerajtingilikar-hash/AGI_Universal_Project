# Cell Biology
Cell biology is the study of the structure, function, and behavior of [[Cells]]. It is a subfield of [[Biology]] that focuses on the cellular level of organization.

## Overview
Cell biology is an interdisciplinary field that combines concepts from [[Molecular Biology]], [[Genetics]], [[Biochemistry]], and [[Physiology]] to understand the complex processes that occur within cells. It involves the study of [[Cell Membrane|cell membranes]], [[Cytoplasm]], [[Nucleus|nuclei]], and other [[Organelles]] that make up a cell. Cell biologists use a range of techniques, including [[Microscopy]], [[Cell Culture]], and [[Molecular Cloning]], to investigate cellular processes such as [[Cell Division]], [[Cell Signaling]], and [[Cell Death]].

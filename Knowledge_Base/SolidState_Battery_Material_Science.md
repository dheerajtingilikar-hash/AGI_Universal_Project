# Solid-State Battery Material Science

## Overview

Solid-state batteries (SSBs) are a type of battery that replaces the liquid electrolyte in traditional lithium-ion batteries with a solid material. This design change aims to improve safety, energy density, and charging speeds. SSBs have the potential to revolutionize the field of battery technology, enabling the widespread adoption of electric vehicles and other high-energy applications.

### Advantages

* Improved safety due to reduced risk of thermal runaway and electrolyte leakage
* Increased energy density and power density
* Faster charging speeds
* Potential for higher operating temperatures

### Challenges

* High material costs and complexity
* Limited understanding of solid-state electrolyte properties and behavior
* Difficulty in scaling up production

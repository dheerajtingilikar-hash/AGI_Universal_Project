# Particle Physics
Particle physics is a branch of [[Physics]] that studies the nature and behavior of [[Subatomic Particles]].

## Overview
Particle physics is concerned with the study of the [[Standard Model]] of particle physics, which describes the behavior of [[Fundamental Particles]] such as [[Quarks]], [[Leptons]], and [[Gauge Bosons]]. The [[Standard Model]] provides a framework for understanding the strong, weak, and electromagnetic [[Fundamental Forces]] that govern the behavior of these particles.

## Concept Map
The following concept map illustrates the key concepts in particle physics:
- [[Particle Physics]]: study of [[Subatomic Particles]]
  - [[Standard Model]]: describes behavior of [[Fundamental Particles]]
    - [[Quarks]]: constituents of [[Hadrons]]
    - [[Leptons]]: charged and neutral particles
    - [[Gauge Bosons]]: particles that mediate [[Fundamental Forces]]
      - [[Photon]]: mediator of [[Electromagnetic Force]]
      - [[Gluon]]: mediator of [[Strong Nuclear Force]]
      - [[W and Z Bosons]]: mediators of [[Weak Nuclear Force]]
  - [[Beyond the Standard Model]]: theories that extend the [[Standard Model]]
    - [[Supersymmetry]]: proposes existence of [[Supersymmetric Particles]]
    - [[Extra Dimensions]]: proposes existence of additional spatial dimensions

## Key Theories and Models
- [[Quantum Field Theory]]: describes behavior of particles in terms of fields
- [[Quantum Electrodynamics]]: describes behavior of [[Electromagnetic Force]]
- [[Quantum Chromodynamics]]: describes behavior of [[Strong Nuclear Force]]

## Experimental Methods
- [[Particle Accelerators]]: used to accelerate particles to high energies
- [[Particle Detectors]]: used to detect and measure properties of particles

## Applications
- [[Medical Imaging]]: uses [[Particle Physics]] to create images of the body
- [[Materials Science]]: uses [[Particle Physics]] to study properties of materials
- [[Energy Applications]]: uses [[Particle Physics]] to develop new energy sources

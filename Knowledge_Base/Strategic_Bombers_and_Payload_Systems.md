# Strategic Bombers and Payload Systems

## Overview

Strategic bombers are aircraft designed to deliver large payloads over long distances, typically to enemy targets such as military installations, industrial centers, or population centers. These aircraft are often equipped with advanced avionics, navigation systems, and defensive countermeasures to ensure successful mission completion.

### Characteristics

- **Payload capacity**: Strategic bombers are designed to carry large payloads, often exceeding 10,000 kg (22,000 lb).
- **Range**: These aircraft have a range of over 8,000 km (5,000 mi), allowing them to reach targets across continents.
- **Speed**: Strategic bombers typically have a cruise speed of 800-900 km/h (500-560 mph).
- **Service ceiling**: These aircraft often have a service ceiling of over 15,000 m (49,000 ft).

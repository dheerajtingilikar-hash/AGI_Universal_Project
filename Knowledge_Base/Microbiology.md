# Microbiology
Microbiology is the scientific study of [[Microorganism|microorganisms]], including [[Bacteria|bacteria]], [[Archaea|archaea]], [[Virus|viruses]], [[Fungus|fungi]], and other microscopic organisms.

## Overview
Microbiology encompasses various subfields, including [[Medical Microbiology]], [[Environmental Microbiology]], and [[Industrial Microbiology]]. It involves the study of the structure, function, and interactions of microorganisms with their environment and other organisms. Microbiologists use various techniques, such as [[Microscopy]], [[Cultivation]], and [[Molecular Biology]], to understand the biology and ecology of microorganisms.

## Concept Map
The field of microbiology is closely related to other areas of biology, including:
* [[Immunology]]: the study of the [[Immune System]] and its interactions with microorganisms
* [[Ecology]]: the study of the relationships between organisms and their environment
* [[Genetics]]: the study of [[Gene|genes]] and their role in the biology of microorganisms
* [[Biochemistry]]: the study of the chemical processes that occur within microorganisms
Key concepts in microbiology include:
* [[Cell Structure]]: the study of the organization and function of cellular components
* [[Metabolism]]: the study of the chemical reactions that occur within microorganisms
* [[Evolution]]: the study of the processes that have shaped the diversity of microorganisms over time

## Subfields
* [[Bacteriology]]: the study of bacteria
* [[Virology]]: the study of viruses
* [[Mycology]]: the study of fungi
* [[Parasitology]]: the study of parasites and their interactions with hosts

## Applications
* [[Public Health]]: the application of microbiology to prevent and control the spread of diseases
* [[Agriculture]]: the use of microbiology to improve crop yields and animal health
* [[Biotechnology]]: the use of microbiology to develop new products and technologies
* [[Environmental Science]]: the study of the impact of microorganisms on the environment and ecosystems.

# Philology
Philology is the study of [[Language]] and [[Linguistics]], focusing on the structure, evolution, and [[Etymology]] of words and languages.

## Overview
[[Philology]] encompasses various disciplines, including [[Historical Linguistics]], [[Comparative Linguistics]], and [[Textual Criticism]]. It involves the analysis of [[Language Families]], [[Dialectology]], and the [[Sociolinguistics]] of language use. [[Philologists]] examine the development of languages, [[Language Change]], and the relationship between languages and cultures.

# Thermodynamics
Thermodynamics is the branch of [[Physics]] that deals with the relationships between [[Heat]], [[Work]], and [[Energy]].

## Overview
Thermodynamics is based on a set of [[Laws of Thermodynamics]] that describe the behavior of [[Thermodynamic Systems]]. The [[Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics]] defines [[Temperature]], while the [[First Law of Thermodynamics]] describes the conservation of [[Energy]]. The [[Second Law of Thermodynamics]] introduces the concept of [[Entropy]], which is a measure of the disorder or randomness of a system. The [[Third Law of Thermodynamics]] provides a fundamental limit on the minimum [[Entropy]] of a system.

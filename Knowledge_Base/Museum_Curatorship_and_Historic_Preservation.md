# Museum Curatorship and Historic Preservation

## Overview

Museum curatorship and historic preservation are closely related fields that involve the management, conservation, and interpretation of cultural and historical artifacts. Museum curators are responsible for acquiring, conserving, and exhibiting artifacts, while historic preservationists focus on protecting and preserving historic buildings, landscapes, and cultural heritage sites.

### Definition

- **Museum Curatorship**: The management and care of museum collections, including acquisition, conservation, and exhibition of artifacts.
- **Historic Preservation**: The protection and conservation of historic buildings, landscapes, and cultural heritage sites.

### Key Concepts

- **Cultural Heritage**: The intangible and tangible aspects of a culture, including artifacts, traditions, and historical sites.
- **Conservation**: The process of preserving and protecting cultural and historical artifacts from deterioration and damage.
- **Exhibition**: The display of artifacts in a museum setting for educational and cultural purposes.
- **Historic District**: A designated area that contains a concentration of historic buildings and cultural heritage sites.
- **Preservation**: The act of protecting and conserving cultural and historical artifacts and sites for future generations.

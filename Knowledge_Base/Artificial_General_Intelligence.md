# Artificial General Intelligence
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) refers to a type of [[Artificial Intelligence]] that possesses the ability to understand, learn, and apply knowledge across a wide range of tasks, similar to human [[Intelligence]].

## Overview
AGI is a hypothetical [[AI]] system that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. It is a long-term goal of [[AI Research]], with the potential to revolutionize numerous fields, including [[Computer Science]], [[Cognitive Science]], and [[Robotics]]. AGI is often considered the holy grail of [[AI]] research, as it would enable machines to think, learn, and adapt like humans.

## Concept Map
The concept of AGI is closely related to other [[AI]] topics, including:
* [[Machine Learning]]: a key component of AGI, which enables machines to learn from data and improve their performance over time.
* [[Natural Language Processing]]: a crucial aspect of AGI, which allows machines to understand and generate human-like language.
* [[Computer Vision]]: a fundamental component of AGI, which enables machines to interpret and understand visual data.
* [[Cognitive Architecture]]: a framework for integrating multiple [[AI]] components to create a cohesive AGI system.
* [[Neural Networks]]: a type of [[Machine Learning]] model that is often used in AGI research, due to its ability to learn complex patterns and relationships in data.

## Key Challenges
The development of AGI is hindered by several challenges, including:
* [[Lack of Understanding]] of human [[Intelligence]] and [[Cognition]]
* [[Scalability]] of current [[AI]] systems
* [[Explainability]] of [[AI]] decisions and actions
* [[Robustness]] and [[Security]] of AGI systems

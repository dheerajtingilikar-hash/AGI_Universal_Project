# Fifth-Generation Jet Fighters

## Overview

Fifth-generation jet fighters are a class of advanced fighter aircraft that incorporate cutting-edge technology, stealth capabilities, and highly integrated avionics systems. These aircraft are designed to provide a significant advantage over fourth-generation fighters in terms of speed, maneuverability, and survivability.

### Characteristics

- **Stealth capabilities**: Fifth-generation fighters feature radar-absorbing materials and designs that reduce their radar cross-section, making them harder to detect.
- **Advanced avionics**: These aircraft are equipped with highly integrated avionics systems, including advanced sensors, data links, and display systems.
- **High-speed performance**: Fifth-generation fighters are designed to achieve high speeds, often exceeding Mach 2.
- **Enhanced maneuverability**: These aircraft feature advanced flight control systems and thrust-vectoring engines, allowing for exceptional agility and maneuverability.

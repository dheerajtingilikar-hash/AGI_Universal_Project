# High Fashion (Haute Couture)
High fashion, also known as [[Haute Couture]], refers to the creation and distribution of exclusive, custom-made clothing and accessories.

## Overview
High fashion is characterized by its emphasis on quality, attention to detail, and use of high-end materials. It is typically designed for special occasions, such as [[Red Carpet]] events, and is often associated with luxury brands like [[Chanel]] and [[Dior]]. The term "haute couture" is protected by law in France, and only a select few fashion houses are granted the official designation.

## Concept Map
* [[Fashion Design]]: the process of creating original clothing and accessory designs
* [[Fashion Week]]: a series of events where designers showcase their latest collections
* [[Runway Model]]: a model who walks the [[Catwalk]] to showcase clothing and accessories
* [[Luxury Brand]]: a brand that is associated with high-end quality and exclusivity
* [[Sustainable Fashion]]: the practice of designing and producing clothing in an environmentally-friendly manner
* [[Fast Fashion]]: the rapid production and distribution of affordable, trendy clothing

## History
High fashion has its roots in 19th-century Europe, where designers like [[Charles Frederick Worth]] created custom-made clothing for the aristocracy. Today, high fashion is a global industry, with designers from around the world contributing to its evolution.

## Notable Designers
* [[Coco Chanel]]: a pioneering designer who revolutionized women's fashion
* [[Christian Dior]]: a legendary designer who founded the iconic fashion house [[Dior]]
* [[Yves Saint Laurent]]: a influential designer who introduced [[Beatnik]] and [[Mod]] styles to high fashion

# Ethics
Ethics refers to the branch of [[Philosophy]] that deals with the study of [[Morality]] and the principles that guide human behavior.

## Overview
Ethics is concerned with the development and application of [[Moral Principles]] that distinguish right from wrong, good from bad, and fair from unfair. It involves the examination of various [[Ethical Theories]] such as [[Consequentialism]], [[Deontology]], and [[Virtue Ethics]]. Ethics is a crucial aspect of numerous fields, including [[Medicine]], [[Law]], [[Business]], and [[Environmental Science]].

## Concept Map
The concept of ethics can be mapped as follows:
- [[Metaethics]]: the study of the nature and scope of ethical statements
- [[Normative Ethics]]: the study of [[Moral Principles]] and [[Ethical Theories]]
- [[Applied Ethics]]: the application of ethical principles to specific fields and issues, such as:
  - [[Bioethics]]: the study of ethical issues in [[Medicine]] and [[Biotechnology]]
  - [[Environmental Ethics]]: the study of ethical issues related to the [[Environment]] and [[Conservation]]
  - [[Professional Ethics]]: the study of ethical issues in [[Professions]] such as [[Law]] and [[Business]]

## Key Terms
- [[Moral Agency]]: the capacity to make [[Moral Decisions]] and act upon them
- [[Moral Responsibility]]: the accountability for one's [[Moral Actions]]
- [[Ethical Dilemma]]: a situation that requires a choice between two or more conflicting [[Moral Principles]]

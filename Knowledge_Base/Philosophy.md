# Philosophy
Philosophy is the study of fundamental nature of [[Knowledge]], [[Reality]], and [[Existence]]. It involves the pursuit of [[Wisdom]] and the examination of [[Ethics]], [[Morality]], and [[Aesthetics]].

## Overview
Philosophy encompasses various branches, including [[Metaphysics]], [[Epistemology]], [[Logic]], [[Ethics]], and [[Aesthetics]]. It explores questions such as the nature of [[Reality]], the limits of [[Knowledge]], and the principles of [[Morality]]. Philosophers use [[Reason]] and [[Argumentation]] to develop and evaluate theories, concepts, and principles.

## Concept Map
The concept map of philosophy includes:
* [[Ontology]]: the study of [[Existence]] and [[Reality]]
* [[Epistemology]]: the study of [[Knowledge]] and [[Belief]]
* [[Logic]]: the study of [[Reasoning]] and [[Argumentation]]
* [[Ethics]]: the study of [[Morality]] and [[Value]]
* [[Aesthetics]]: the study of [[Beauty]] and [[Taste]]
* [[Philosophy of Mind]]: the study of [[Consciousness]] and [[Mental States]]
* [[Philosophy of Language]]: the study of [[Language]] and [[Meaning]]

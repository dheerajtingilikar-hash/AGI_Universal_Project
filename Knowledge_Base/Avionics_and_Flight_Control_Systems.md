# Avionics and Flight Control Systems

## Overview

Avionics and flight control systems are critical components of modern aircraft, responsible for ensuring safe and efficient flight operations. The term "avionics" refers to the electronic systems used in aircraft, while "flight control systems" encompasses the mechanisms that control the aircraft's movement and stability.

### Definition

Avionics: The branch of electronics that deals with the design, development, and operation of electronic systems used in aircraft.

Flight Control Systems: A system of mechanical, hydraulic, or electronic components that control the movement and stability of an aircraft.

### Key Components

- **Flight Control Surfaces**: Ailerons, elevators, and rudder, which control roll, pitch, and yaw.
- **Flight Control Computers**: Electronic systems that process data from sensors and control the flight control surfaces.
- **Autopilot Systems**: Automated systems that control the aircraft's flight path and altitude.
- **Flight Management Systems**: Computer systems that manage flight planning, navigation, and communication.

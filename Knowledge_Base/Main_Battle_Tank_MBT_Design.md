# Main Battle Tank (MBT) Design

## Overview

The Main Battle Tank (MBT) is a type of armored fighting vehicle designed for frontline combat. MBTs are typically equipped with a main gun, secondary armaments, and advanced armor to provide protection against enemy fire. The design of an MBT involves a combination of mobility, firepower, and survivability.

### Characteristics

- **Weight**: Typically ranges from 40 to 70 tons
- **Length**: Varies between 6 to 10 meters
- **Width**: Typically ranges from 3 to 4 meters
- **Height**: Typically ranges from 2 to 3 meters
- **Top Speed**: Can reach speeds of up to 70 km/h
- **Range**: Can travel up to 500 km without refueling

### Mobility

- **Engine**: Typically a diesel or gas turbine engine
- **Transmission**: Automatic or semi-automatic transmission
- **Suspension**: Torsion bar or hydraulic suspension

### Firepower

- **Main Gun**: Typically a 120mm smoothbore cannon
- **Secondary Armaments**: Machine guns or anti-tank guided missiles
- **Ammunition**: Various types of ammunition, including armor-piercing and high-explosive rounds

### Survivability

- **Armor**: Composite armor, including ceramic and metal plates
- **Countermeasures**: Smoke dischargers, active protection systems, and camouflage

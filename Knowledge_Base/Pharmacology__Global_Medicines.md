# Pharmacology & Global Medicines

## Overview

Pharmacology and global medicines is a multidisciplinary field that combines the study of drugs, their properties, and their effects on living organisms with the global distribution and accessibility of medicines. This field encompasses various aspects, including the discovery, development, and regulation of pharmaceuticals, as well as their use in different cultural and socioeconomic contexts.

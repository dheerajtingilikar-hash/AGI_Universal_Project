# Physiology
Physiology is the scientific study of [[Function (biology)|functions]] and [[Mechanism (biology)|mechanisms]] that occur within a living [[Organism|organism]]. 

## Overview
Physiology is a branch of [[Biology]] that focuses on the internal functions of living organisms, including the [[Cell (biology)|cells]], [[Tissue (biology)|tissues]], and [[Organ (biology)|organs]] that make up the organism. It examines the physical and chemical processes that occur within an organism to maintain [[Homeostasis|homeostasis]] and overall health. The field of physiology is closely related to other fields such as [[Anatomy]], [[Biochemistry]], and [[Neuroscience]].

## Concept Map
The study of physiology can be divided into several subfields, including:
* [[Neurophysiology]]: the study of the [[Nervous system|nervous system]] and its functions
* [[Cardiovascular physiology]]: the study of the [[Circulatory system|circulatory system]] and its functions
* [[Respiratory physiology]]: the study of the [[Respiratory system|respiratory system]] and its functions
* [[Endocrinology]]: the study of the [[Endocrine system|endocrine system]] and its functions
* [[Exercise physiology]]: the study of the physiological responses to [[Exercise|exercise]] and physical activity

These subfields are interconnected and work together to maintain the overall health and function of the organism. Understanding physiology is essential for the diagnosis and treatment of various diseases and disorders, and is a crucial aspect of [[Medicine]] and [[Healthcare]].

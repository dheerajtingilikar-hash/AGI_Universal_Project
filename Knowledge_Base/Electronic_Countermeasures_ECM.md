# Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)

## Overview

Electronic Countermeasures (ECM) are techniques used to disrupt, deceive, or destroy enemy radar and communication systems. ECM involves the use of electromagnetic radiation to interfere with or destroy the functionality of an adversary's electronic systems.

### Types of ECM

- [[Active ECM]]: Involves the transmission of electromagnetic radiation to interfere with or destroy enemy systems.
- [[Passive ECM]]: Involves the use of natural or artificial sources of electromagnetic radiation to interfere with or destroy enemy systems.
- [[Electronic Support Measures (ESM)]]: Involves the detection and analysis of enemy electronic emissions to identify and locate enemy systems.

### Applications of ECM

- [[Military]]: ECM is used to disrupt enemy radar and communication systems, providing a tactical advantage in combat situations.
- [[Aviation]]: ECM is used to protect aircraft from enemy radar and missile systems.
- [[Cyber Warfare]]: ECM is used to disrupt or destroy enemy computer systems and networks.

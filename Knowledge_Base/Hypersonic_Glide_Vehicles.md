# Hypersonic Glide Vehicles

## Overview

Hypersonic Glide Vehicles (HGVs) are unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) designed to operate at speeds above Mach 5, typically in the range of Mach 5-25. They are propelled by a rocket or scramjet (supersonic combustion ramjet) engine, and are often used for reconnaissance, strike, and anti-satellite missions.

### Characteristics

- **Speed**: Hypersonic speeds, typically above Mach 5
- **Range**: Variable, up to several thousand kilometers
- **Payload**: Limited, often focused on reconnaissance or strike capabilities
- **Propulsion**: Rocket or scramjet engines
- **Control**: Often uses aerodynamic control surfaces or reaction control systems

## Types

- **[[Scramjet]]-powered HGVs**: Use a scramjet engine to achieve hypersonic speeds
- **[[Rocket]]-powered HGVs**: Use a rocket engine to achieve hypersonic speeds
- **[[Air-breathing]] HGVs**: Use atmospheric oxygen to sustain combustion in a scramjet engine

## Applications

- **Reconnaissance**: HGVs can be used to gather intelligence on enemy territory
- **Strike**: HGVs can be used to deliver precision-guided munitions
- **Anti-Satellite**: HGVs can be used to intercept and destroy satellites

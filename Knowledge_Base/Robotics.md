# Robotics
## Overview
Robotics is an interdisciplinary field that combines [[Computer Science]], [[Engineering]], and [[Mathematics]] to design, build, and operate [[Robot]]s. It involves the development of intelligent machines that can perform tasks autonomously or with human interaction. Robotics has numerous applications in industries such as [[Manufacturing]], [[Healthcare]], and [[Transportation]].

## Concept Map
The field of robotics can be divided into several key areas, including:
* [[Artificial Intelligence]]: the development of algorithms and techniques that enable robots to learn, reason, and interact with their environment
* [[Machine Learning]]: a subset of artificial intelligence that involves the use of data and statistical models to improve robot performance
* [[Computer Vision]]: the use of cameras and sensors to enable robots to perceive and understand their environment
* [[Robotics Engineering]]: the design, development, and testing of robotic systems, including [[Mechanical Engineering]], [[Electrical Engineering]], and [[Software Engineering]]
* [[Human-Robot Interaction]]: the study of how humans interact with robots, including [[User Experience]] and [[User Interface]] design

## Key Technologies
* [[Sensors]]: devices that enable robots to perceive their environment, such as [[Camera]]s, [[Lidar]]s, and [[GPS]]
* [[Actuators]]: devices that enable robots to move and interact with their environment, such as [[Motors]] and [[Pumps]]
* [[Microcontrollers]]: small computers that control and coordinate robot movements and actions

## Applications
* [[Industrial Robotics]]: the use of robots in manufacturing and production, including [[Welding]], [[Assembly]], and [[Inspection]]
* [[Service Robotics]]: the use of robots in service industries, including [[Healthcare Robotics]], [[Domestic Robotics]], and [[Agricultural Robotics]]
* [[Autonomous Vehicles]]: the use of robots in transportation, including [[Self-Driving Cars]] and [[Drones]]

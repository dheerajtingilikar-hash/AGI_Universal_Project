# Aerospace Engineering
Aerospace Engineering is the primary field of engineering concerned with the development of [[Aircraft]] and [[Spacecraft]]. It is a multidisciplinary field that combines aspects of [[Mechanical Engineering]] and [[Electrical Engineering]] to design and manufacture vehicles that can withstand the extreme conditions of flight.

## Overview
Aerospace Engineering is a broad field that encompasses various disciplines, including [[Aerodynamics]], [[Propulsion Systems]], [[Structural Analysis]], and [[Control Systems]]. Aerospace engineers design and develop aircraft, spacecraft, and missiles, as well as [[Satellites]] and other space-related systems. The field requires a strong foundation in mathematics and physics, as well as expertise in materials science and computer-aided design.

## Concept Map
The following concept map illustrates the key areas of Aerospace Engineering:
- [[Aerodynamics]]: study of the interaction between air and solid objects, such as aircraft and spacecraft
- [[Propulsion Systems]]: design and development of systems that generate thrust, including [[Jet Engines]] and [[Rocket Engines]]
- [[Structural Analysis]]: analysis of the stress and strain on aircraft and spacecraft structures, including [[Materials Science]] and [[Finite Element Analysis]]
- [[Control Systems]]: design and development of systems that control the flight trajectory of aircraft and spacecraft, including [[Autopilot Systems]] and [[Navigation Systems]]
- [[Spacecraft Operations]]: planning and execution of space missions, including [[Launch Vehicles]] and [[Orbit Determination]]

## Subfields
Aerospace Engineering has several subfields, including:
- [[Aeronautical Engineering]]: design and development of aircraft and helicopters
- [[Astrodynamics]]: study of the motion of spacecraft and celestial bodies
- [[Rocket Science]]: design and development of rockets and missile systems

## Applications
Aerospace Engineering has numerous applications in:
- [[Aviation Industry]]: design and development of commercial and military aircraft
- [[Space Exploration]]: design and development of spacecraft and space-related systems
- [[Defense Industry]]: design and development of military aircraft and missile systems

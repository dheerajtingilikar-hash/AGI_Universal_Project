# The Industrial Revolution
The Industrial Revolution was a transformative period in human history, marking the shift from manual labor to machine-based manufacturing and changing the global economy forever.

## Overview
The Industrial Revolution began in the late 18th century in [[Britain]] and spread to other parts of the world, including [[Europe]] and [[North America]]. It was characterized by the development of new [[Technologies]], such as the [[Steam Engine]] and the [[Spinning Jenny]], which enabled mass production and increased efficiency. The revolution also saw the growth of [[Factories]] and the development of new [[Transportation]] systems, including [[Railroads]] and [[Canals]].

## Key Terms
* [[Industrial Revolution]]: a period of significant economic and social change
* [[Mass Production]]: the production of large quantities of goods using machine-based manufacturing
* [[Factory System]]: a system of production where goods are manufactured in a central location using machine-based manufacturing

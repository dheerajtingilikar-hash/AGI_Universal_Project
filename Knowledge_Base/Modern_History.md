# Modern History
Modern History refers to the period of [[Human History]] from the [[Renaissance]] to the present day. It is characterized by significant events, cultural movements, and technological advancements that have shaped the world as we know it today.

## Overview
Modern History is typically divided into several distinct periods, including the [[Age of Exploration]], the [[Industrial Revolution]], and the [[Digital Age]]. Each of these periods has been marked by major developments in [[Politics]], [[Economy]], [[Science]], and [[Technology]]. The [[World Wars]] and the [[Cold War]] have also played a significant role in shaping the course of Modern History.

## Concept Map
The key concepts in Modern History can be organized into the following categories:
* [[Historical Events]]: [[French Revolution]], [[World War I]], [[World War II]], [[Cold War]]
* [[Cultural Movements]]: [[Renaissance Humanism]], [[Enlightenment]], [[Romanticism]], [[Modernism]]
* [[Technological Advancements]]: [[Printing Press]], [[Steam Engine]], [[Internet]], [[Artificial Intelligence]]
* [[Global Issues]]: [[Imperialism]], [[Colonization]], [[Globalization]], [[Climate Change]]

These concepts are interconnected and have influenced one another in complex ways, shaping the course of Modern History and continuing to impact the world today.

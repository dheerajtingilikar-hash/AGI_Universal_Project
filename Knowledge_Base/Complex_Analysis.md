# Complex Analysis
Complex analysis is a branch of [[Mathematics]] that deals with the study of [[Complex Numbers]] and their properties. It is a fundamental subject that has numerous applications in [[Physics]], [[Engineering]], and other fields.

## Overview
Complex analysis is concerned with the study of [[Analytic Functions]], which are functions that can be represented by a [[Power Series]] in the [[Complex Plane]]. The subject involves the study of [[Holomorphic Functions]], [[Meromorphic Functions]], and [[Entire Functions]], among others. Complex analysis has numerous applications in [[Signal Processing]], [[Control Theory]], and [[Quantum Mechanics]].

## Concept Map
The key concepts in complex analysis can be organized into the following concept map:
- [[Complex Numbers]]: The basic objects of study in complex analysis
- [[Analytic Functions]]: Functions that can be represented by a [[Power Series]] in the [[Complex Plane]]
- [[Holomorphic Functions]]: Functions that are [[Differentiable]] in the [[Complex Plane]]
- [[Meromorphic Functions]]: Functions that are [[Holomorphic]] except at a set of isolated [[Poles]]
- [[Entire Functions]]: Functions that are [[Holomorphic]] everywhere in the [[Complex Plane]]
- [[Residue Theorem]]: A theorem that relates the value of a [[Contour Integral]] to the [[Residues]] of the function at its [[Poles]]
- [[Cauchy-Riemann Equations]]: A set of equations that characterize [[Holomorphic Functions]] in terms of their [[Partial Derivatives]]

## Key Theorems
- [[Cauchy's Integral Theorem]]: A theorem that states that a [[Contour Integral]] of a [[Holomorphic Function]] is zero if the contour is [[Simply Connected]]
- [[Cauchy's Integral Formula]]: A theorem that relates the value of a [[Holomorphic Function]] to its [[Contour Integral]]
- [[Residue Theorem]]: A theorem that relates the value of a [[Contour Integral]] to the [[Residues]] of the function at its [[Poles]]

## Applications
- [[Signal Processing]]: Complex analysis is used in signal processing to analyze and filter signals
- [[Control Theory]]: Complex analysis is used in control theory to design and analyze control systems
- [[Quantum Mechanics]]: Complex analysis is used in quantum mechanics to solve the [[Schrodinger Equation]] and study the behavior of quantum systems.

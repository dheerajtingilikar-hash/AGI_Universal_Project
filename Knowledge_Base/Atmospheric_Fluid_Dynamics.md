# Atmospheric Fluid Dynamics
Atmospheric fluid dynamics is the study of the movement and behavior of [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] fluids, including [[Air|air]] and other gases. It is a subfield of [[Fluid Dynamics|fluid dynamics]] that focuses on the unique properties and characteristics of atmospheric fluids.

## Overview
Atmospheric fluid dynamics involves the application of [[Physics|physical]] principles to understand the motion of [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] fluids, including the effects of [[Temperature|temperature]], [[Humidity|humidity]], and [[Pressure|pressure]]. It is used to study a wide range of phenomena, including [[Weather|weather]] patterns, [[Climate|climate]] trends, and [[Atmospheric Circulation|atmospheric circulation]].

## Concept Map
The key concepts in atmospheric fluid dynamics include:
* [[Fluid Mechanics|Fluid mechanics]]: the study of the behavior of fluids under various forces and conditions
* [[Thermodynamics|Thermodynamics]]: the study of the relationships between [[Heat|heat]], [[Work|work]], and [[Energy|energy]]
* [[Aerodynamics|Aerodynamics]]: the study of the interaction between [[Air|air]] and solid objects
* [[Meteorology|Meteorology]]: the study of the [[Atmosphere|atmosphere]] and [[Weather|weather]] phenomena
* [[Climate Science|Climate science]]: the study of long-term [[Climate|climate]] trends and patterns

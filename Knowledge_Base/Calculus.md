# Calculus
Calculus is a branch of [[Mathematics]] that deals with the study of continuous change, particularly in the context of [[Functions]] and [[Limits]].

## Overview
Calculus is divided into two main branches: [[Differential Calculus]] and [[Integral Calculus]]. [[Differential Calculus]] studies the rates of change and slopes of curves, while [[Integral Calculus]] studies the accumulation of quantities. Calculus has numerous applications in [[Physics]], [[Engineering]], [[Economics]], and [[Computer Science]].

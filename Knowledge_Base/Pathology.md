# Pathology
Pathology is the scientific study of [[Disease]] and its underlying causes. It involves the examination of [[Tissue]] and [[Cell]] samples to diagnose and understand various diseases.

## Overview
Pathology is a branch of [[Medicine]] that deals with the diagnosis and study of diseases. It involves the use of various techniques such as [[Histopathology]], [[Cytology]], and [[Molecular Pathology]] to examine and analyze tissue and cell samples. Pathologists use this information to diagnose diseases, determine the cause of death, and develop treatment plans.

## Concept Map
The field of pathology can be divided into several subfields, including:
* [[Anatomical Pathology]]: the study of the structure and organization of tissues and cells
* [[Clinical Pathology]]: the study of the laboratory analysis of bodily fluids and tissues
* [[Molecular Pathology]]: the study of the molecular mechanisms underlying disease
* [[Surgical Pathology]]: the study of the diagnosis and treatment of diseases through surgical means
Pathology is closely related to other fields such as [[Oncology]], [[Pharmacology]], and [[Genetics]]. It plays a crucial role in the diagnosis and treatment of various diseases, including [[Cancer]], [[Infectious Disease]], and [[Genetic Disorder]].

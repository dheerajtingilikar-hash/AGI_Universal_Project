# Meteorology
Meteorology is the scientific study of the [[Atmosphere]] and its phenomena, including [[Weather]] and [[Climate]].

## Overview
Meteorology involves the observation, analysis, and forecasting of atmospheric conditions, such as [[Temperature]], [[Humidity]], [[Atmospheric Pressure]], and [[Wind]]. It also encompasses the study of [[Meteorological Phenomena]], including [[Clouds]], [[Precipitation]], and [[Severe Weather Events]] like [[Tornadoes]] and [[Hurricanes]].

## Concept Map
The field of meteorology is closely related to other scientific disciplines, including:
* [[Climatology]]: the study of long-term atmospheric conditions and trends
* [[Atmospheric Science]]: the study of the Earth's atmosphere and its interactions with the [[Hydrosphere]] and [[Lithosphere]]
* [[Hydrology]]: the study of the Earth's water cycle and its interactions with the atmosphere
* [[Geophysics]]: the study of the Earth's physical properties and processes, including those related to the atmosphere

Key concepts in meteorology include:
* [[Atmospheric Circulation]]: the movement of air in the atmosphere, including [[Wind Patterns]] and [[Ocean Currents]]
* [[Weather Forecasting]]: the use of [[Computer Models]] and [[Observational Data]] to predict future weather conditions
* [[Climate Modeling]]: the use of [[Computer Simulations]] to study and predict long-term climate trends and patterns.

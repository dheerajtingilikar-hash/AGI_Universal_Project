# Human Physical Performance and Recreation

## Overview

Human physical performance and recreation refer to the various aspects of human physical activity, including exercise, sports, and leisure activities. This encompasses the physiological, psychological, and biomechanical factors that influence human movement and performance.

### Definition

Human physical performance and recreation involve the interaction between the human body and its environment, with a focus on the physical and psychological aspects of movement and activity.

### Scope

This topic includes:

- Exercise science
- Sports performance
- Biomechanics
- Motor control
- Physical education
- Recreation and leisure activities

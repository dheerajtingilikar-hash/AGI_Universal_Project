# Mathematics
## Overview
Mathematics is the study of numbers, quantities, and shapes. It involves the use of [[Algebra]], [[Geometry]], and [[Calculus]] to analyze and solve problems. Mathematics is a fundamental subject that underlies many fields, including [[Physics]], [[Engineering]], and [[Computer Science]].

## Concept Map
* [[Number Theory]]: The study of properties of integers and other whole numbers.
* [[Combinatorics]]: The study of counting and arranging objects in various ways.
* [[Graph Theory]]: The study of graphs, which are collections of nodes connected by edges.
* [[Topology]]: The study of shapes and spaces, including their properties and transformations.
* [[Probability Theory]]: The study of chance events and their likelihood of occurrence.
* [[Statistics]]: The study of the collection, analysis, and interpretation of data.

## Branches of Mathematics
### Pure Mathematics
* [[Algebraic Geometry]]: The study of geometric objects using algebraic techniques.
* [[Number Theory]]: The study of properties of integers and other whole numbers.
* [[Topology]]: The study of shapes and spaces, including their properties and transformations.

### Applied Mathematics
* [[Differential Equations]]: The study of equations that describe how quantities change over time.
* [[Numerical Analysis]]: The study of algorithms for solving mathematical problems using numerical methods.
* [[Optimization]]: The study of finding the best solution among a set of possible solutions.

## Related Fields
* [[Computer Science]]: The study of computers, algorithms, and data structures.
* [[Physics]]: The study of the natural world, including the laws of motion and energy.
* [[Engineering]]: The application of mathematical and scientific principles to design and build structures and machines.

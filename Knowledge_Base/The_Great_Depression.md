# The Great Depression
The Great Depression was a severe global [[Economic downturn]] that occurred from 1929 to the late 1930s.

## Overview
The Great Depression was triggered by the [[Stock market crash of 1929]], which led to a massive loss of [[Wealth]] and a significant decline in [[International trade]]. The crisis affected many countries, including the [[United States]], [[Canada]], and [[Europe]]. The effects of the Great Depression were widespread, with high levels of [[Unemployment]], [[Poverty]], and [[Homelessness]].

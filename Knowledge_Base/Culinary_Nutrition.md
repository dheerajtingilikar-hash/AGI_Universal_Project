# Culinary Nutrition
Culinary nutrition is the study of the relationship between [[Food]], [[Nutrition]], and [[Health]]. It involves the application of [[Nutritional Science]] to the preparation and consumption of [[Cuisine]].

## Overview
Culinary nutrition is an interdisciplinary field that combines [[Food Science]], [[Nutrition Science]], and [[Culinary Arts]]. It aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the nutritional value of [[Food]] and its impact on human [[Health]]. Culinary nutritionists use their knowledge to develop [[Healthy Eating]] plans, create [[Nutritious Recipes]], and promote [[Sustainable Food Systems]].

## Concept Map
The concept of culinary nutrition can be mapped as follows:
- [[Food]] → [[Nutrition]] → [[Health]]
- [[Culinary Arts]] → [[Food Preparation]] → [[Food Science]]
- [[Nutrition Science]] → [[Nutrient Analysis]] → [[Diet Planning]]
- [[Sustainable Food Systems]] → [[Food Production]] → [[Food Distribution]]
- [[Healthy Eating]] → [[Balanced Diet]] → [[Disease Prevention]]

## Key Concepts
- [[Macronutrients]]: [[Carbohydrates]], [[Proteins]], and [[Fats]]
- [[Micronutrients]]: [[Vitamins]] and [[Minerals]]
- [[Dietary Guidelines]]: recommendations for [[Healthy Eating]]
- [[Food Safety]]: handling and preparation of [[Food]] to prevent [[Foodborne Illness]]

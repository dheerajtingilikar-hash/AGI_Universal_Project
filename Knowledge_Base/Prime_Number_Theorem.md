# Prime Number Theorem
The Prime Number Theorem (PNT) is a fundamental result in [[Number Theory]], describing the distribution of [[Prime Number]]s among the [[Integer]]s.

## Overview
The Prime Number Theorem states that the number of prime numbers less than or equal to $x$, denoted by $\pi(x)$, is approximately equal to $\frac{x}{\ln(x)}$ as $x$ approaches infinity. This theorem provides a precise estimate of the distribution of prime numbers, which is essential in many areas of mathematics, including [[Cryptography]] and [[Computer Science]].

## History
The Prime Number Theorem was first proposed by [[Carl Friedrich Gauss]] in the early 19th century and was later proved by [[Hadamar]] and [[de la Vallée Poussin]] in the late 19th century.

# Constitutional Frameworks of the 195 Sovereign States

## Overview

The constitutional frameworks of the 195 sovereign states vary significantly, reflecting the unique historical, cultural, and political contexts of each nation. This entry provides an overview of the different types of constitutional frameworks found in these states.

### Types of Constitutional Frameworks

- **Constitutional Monarchies**: [[Constitutional Monarchy]] - 43 states, including the United Kingdom, Japan, and Sweden.
- **Presidential Systems**: [[Presidential System]] - 94 states, including the United States, Russia, and China.
- **Parliamentary Systems**: [[Parliamentary System]] - 48 states, including the United Kingdom, Canada, and India.
- **Federal Systems**: [[Federal System]] - 27 states, including the United States, Germany, and Australia.
- **Unitary Systems**: [[Unitary System]] - 33 states, including France, Japan, and Sweden.
- **Theocratic Systems**: [[Theocratic System]] - 5 states, including the Vatican City and Iran.
- **Authoritarian Systems**: [[Authoritarian System]] - 12 states, including North Korea and Saudi Arabia.

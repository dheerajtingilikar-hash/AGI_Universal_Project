# Paleontology
Paleontology is the scientific study of [[Fossil]]s and ancient life forms. It is a multidisciplinary field that combines [[Geology]], [[Biology]], and [[Ecology]] to understand the history of life on [[Earth]].

## Overview
Paleontology involves the discovery, excavation, and analysis of fossils, which are the preserved remains or traces of ancient organisms. The field encompasses the study of [[Paleozoology]] (ancient animals), [[Paleobotany]] (ancient plants), and [[Micropaleontology]] (ancient microorganisms). Paleontologists use various techniques, including [[Radiometric Dating]] and [[Biostratigraphy]], to reconstruct the [[Geologic Time Scale]] and understand the evolution of life on Earth.

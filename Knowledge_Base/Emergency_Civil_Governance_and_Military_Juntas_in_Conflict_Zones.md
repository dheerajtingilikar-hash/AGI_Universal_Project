# Emergency Civil Governance and Military Juntas in Conflict Zones

## Overview

Emergency civil governance and military juntas in conflict zones refer to the temporary or permanent takeover of power by military forces or emergency civilian administrations in areas affected by conflict, instability, or crisis. This phenomenon often arises when a country's government is unable to maintain order, and the military or emergency authorities assume control to maintain stability and security.

### Characteristics

- **Definition**: A temporary or permanent takeover of power by military forces or emergency civilian administrations in conflict zones.
- **Causes**: Government instability, conflict, crisis, or inability to maintain order.
- **Effects**: Maintenance of stability and security, potential human rights abuses, and long-term impact on governance and democracy.

## Types of Emergency Civil Governance

### 1. **Military Junta**

A military junta is a group of military officers who take control of a government, often in times of crisis or conflict. Examples include:

- **[[Military coup]]**: A sudden overthrow of a government by military force.
- **[[Authoritarian regime]]**: A government that exercises total control over citizens, often through military force.

### 2. **Emergency Civil Administration**

An emergency civil administration is a temporary government established to manage a crisis or conflict. Examples include:

- **[[Humanitarian intervention]]**: Military intervention to protect civilians from harm.
- **[[Occupation]]**: Military control of a territory or country, often following a conflict.

## Case Studies

- **[[Sierra Leone]]**: The Armed Forces Revolutionary Council (AFRC) took control of the government in 1997, leading to a civil war.
- **[[Haiti]]**: The military junta, led by Raoul Cédras, took control of the government in 1991, leading to a humanitarian crisis.
- **[[Somalia]]**: The Transitional Federal Government (TFG) was established in 2004 to manage the country's transition to democracy.

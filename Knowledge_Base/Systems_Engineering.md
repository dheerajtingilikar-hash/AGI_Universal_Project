# Systems Engineering
Systems Engineering is an interdisciplinary field of [[Engineering]] that focuses on the development and implementation of complex systems. It involves the application of [[Mathematics]], [[Science]], and [[Technology]] to design, build, and maintain systems that meet specific requirements and performance standards.

## Overview
Systems Engineering is a holistic approach that considers the entire system lifecycle, from [[Conceptualization]] to [[Deployment]] and [[Maintenance]]. It involves the integration of multiple disciplines, including [[Computer Science]], [[Mechanical Engineering]], [[Electrical Engineering]], and [[Software Engineering]]. The goal of Systems Engineering is to create systems that are reliable, efficient, and effective in achieving their intended purpose.

## Concept Map
The Systems Engineering concept map includes the following key elements:
* [[System Requirements]]: The definition of the system's functional and performance requirements
* [[System Design]]: The creation of a detailed design of the system, including its architecture and components
* [[System Integration]]: The process of combining the system's components into a cohesive whole
* [[System Testing]]: The evaluation of the system's performance and functionality
* [[System Maintenance]]: The ongoing support and maintenance of the system to ensure its continued operation and performance
* [[Systems Thinking]]: The consideration of the system as a whole, including its interactions and interdependencies with other systems and stakeholders

## Key Principles
The key principles of Systems Engineering include:
* [[Holism]]: The consideration of the system as a whole, rather than just its individual components
* [[Interdisciplinarity]]: The integration of multiple disciplines and perspectives to create a comprehensive understanding of the system
* [[Lifecycle Thinking]]: The consideration of the entire system lifecycle, from conceptualization to deployment and maintenance
* [[Risk Management]]: The identification and mitigation of risks and uncertainties that could impact the system's performance and success

# Material Science
Material science is the study of the properties and applications of various materials, including [[Metals]], [[Polymers]], and [[Ceramics]].

## Overview
Material science is an interdisciplinary field that combines principles from [[Physics]], [[Chemistry]], and [[Engineering]] to understand the structure, properties, and performance of materials. It involves the development of new materials and the improvement of existing ones, with applications in a wide range of fields, including [[Aerospace Engineering]], [[Biomedical Engineering]], and [[Energy Storage]].

## Concept Map
The field of material science can be divided into several key areas, including:
* [[Materials Synthesis]]: the process of creating new materials with specific properties
* [[Materials Characterization]]: the use of techniques such as [[X-Ray Diffraction]] and [[Scanning Electron Microscopy]] to determine the structure and properties of materials
* [[Mechanical Properties]]: the study of the strength, [[Stiffness]], and [[Ductility]] of materials
* [[Thermal Properties]]: the study of the [[Thermal Conductivity]] and [[Specific Heat]] of materials
* [[Electrical Properties]]: the study of the [[Electrical Conductivity]] and [[Dielectric Properties]] of materials

Related fields include:
* [[Nanotechnology]]
* [[Biomaterials]]
* [[Composites]]

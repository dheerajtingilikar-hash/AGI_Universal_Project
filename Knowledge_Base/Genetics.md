# Genetics
Genetics is the scientific study of [[Hereditary|heredity]] and [[Variation|variation]].

## Overview
Genetics is a branch of [[Biology]] that deals with the study of [[Genes|genes]], [[Genetic Variation|genetic variation]], and [[Hereditary|heredity]]. It involves the study of the structure and function of [[DNA|DNA]], [[RNA|RNA]], and [[Proteins|proteins]], as well as the mechanisms of [[Genetic Inheritance|genetic inheritance]]. Genetics is a fundamental discipline that underlies many fields, including [[Medicine]], [[Agriculture]], and [[Biotechnology]].

## Concept Map
The field of genetics can be divided into several subfields, including:
* [[Molecular Genetics]]: the study of the structure and function of [[DNA|DNA]] and [[RNA|RNA]]
* [[Population Genetics]]: the study of [[Genetic Variation|genetic variation]] in [[Population|populations]]
* [[Quantitative Genetics]]: the study of the inheritance of [[Quantitative Trait|quantitative traits]]
* [[Epigenetics]]: the study of [[Epigenetic|epigenetic]] modifications and their role in [[Gene Expression|gene expression]]
* [[Genomics]]: the study of the structure, function, and evolution of [[Genome|genomes]]

# Psychology
Psychology is the [[Scientific Study]] of [[Behavior]] and [[Mental Processes]]. It aims to understand individuals and [[Group Behavior]], exploring underlying psychological processes and mechanisms.

## Overview
Psychology is a diverse field, encompassing various [[Subdisciplines]] such as [[Clinical Psychology]], [[Cognitive Psychology]], [[Developmental Psychology]], and [[Social Psychology]]. It seeks to understand [[Emotion]], [[Motivation]], [[Perception]], and [[Cognition]], among other psychological phenomena. Psychology has numerous [[Applications]], including [[Mental Health]] treatment, [[Education]], and [[Industry]].

## Concept Map
The field of psychology is interconnected with other disciplines, including:
* [[Biology]]: studying the [[Neurological]] basis of behavior
* [[Philosophy]]: exploring the nature of [[Consciousness]] and [[Free Will]]
* [[Sociology]]: examining the impact of [[Social Environment]] on behavior
* [[Anthropology]]: investigating [[Cultural Differences]] in psychological processes
Key concepts in psychology include:
* [[Learning Theory]]
* [[Personality Theory]]
* [[Intelligence Quotient]] (IQ)
* [[Attachment Theory]]

# Advanced Calculus
Advanced Calculus is a branch of [[Mathematics]] that deals with the study of [[Limits]], [[Derivatives]], and [[Integrals]] of [[Functions]] of multiple [[Variables]]. It is a fundamental subject that has numerous applications in [[Physics]], [[Engineering]], and other fields.

## Overview
Advanced Calculus is an extension of [[Calculus]], which introduces more advanced techniques and concepts, such as [[Multivariable Calculus]], [[Differential Equations]], and [[Vector Analysis]]. It provides a framework for modeling and analyzing complex phenomena, and is a crucial tool for scientists and engineers.

## Concept Map
The key concepts in Advanced Calculus can be organized into the following categories:
* [[Multivariable Calculus]]: deals with the study of functions of multiple variables, including [[Partial Derivatives]] and [[Multiple Integrals]]
* [[Differential Equations]]: involves the study of equations that describe how quantities change over time or space, including [[Ordinary Differential Equations]] and [[Partial Differential Equations]]
* [[Vector Analysis]]: includes the study of [[Vector Fields]], [[Line Integrals]], and [[Surface Integrals]]
* [[Topology]]: is the study of the properties of shapes and spaces that are preserved under continuous deformations, including [[Connectedness]] and [[Compactness]]

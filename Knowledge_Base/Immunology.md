# Immunology
Immunology is the branch of [[Biology]] that focuses on the study of the [[Immune System]] and its responses to [[Pathogen]]s, [[Disease]]s, and other foreign substances.

## Overview
Immunology is an interdisciplinary field that combines concepts from [[Molecular Biology]], [[Cell Biology]], [[Genetics]], and [[Biochemistry]] to understand the complex interactions between the immune system and its environment. The immune system is composed of multiple [[Cell Type]]s, including [[T Cell]]s, [[B Cell]]s, and [[Macrophage]]s, which work together to protect the body against [[Infection]] and [[Disease]].

## Concept Map
The concept map of immunology includes the following key topics:
* [[Innate Immunity]]: the first line of defense against infection, mediated by [[Pattern Recognition Receptor]]s and [[Inflammatory Response]]s
* [[Adaptive Immunity]]: the specific response to infection, mediated by [[T Cell]]s and [[B Cell]]s
* [[Immune Tolerance]]: the ability of the immune system to distinguish between self and non-self, and to avoid [[Autoimmune Disease]]
* [[Vaccine]]s: substances that stimulate the immune system to produce a specific response to a [[Pathogen]]
* [[Immunotherapy]]: the use of [[Immune System]]-modulating treatments to prevent or treat [[Disease]]

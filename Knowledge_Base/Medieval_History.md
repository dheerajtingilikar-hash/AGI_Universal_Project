# Medieval History
Medieval History refers to the period of European history from the 5th to the 15th century, spanning the [[Middle Ages]]. This era is characterized by the rise and fall of various [[Empire]]s, the growth of [[Feudalism]], and the development of distinct [[Culture]]s.

## Overview
The Medieval period is typically divided into three main sections: the [[Early Middle Ages]], the [[High Middle Ages]], and the [[Late Middle Ages]]. The Early Middle Ages saw the rise of [[Charlemagne]] and the [[Carolingian Empire]], while the High Middle Ages were marked by the growth of [[Trade]] and the development of [[University]]es. The Late Middle Ages were characterized by the [[Black Death]] and the [[Hundred Years' War]].

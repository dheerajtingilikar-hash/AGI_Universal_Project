# Tactical Brief 2026-04-20 21-35-39

## Overview

This tactical brief outlines a comprehensive strategy for a hypothetical military operation. The operation aims to neutralize a high-value target (HVT) in a hostile environment.

### Situation

* **Location**: The HVT is located in a densely populated urban area with limited access points.
* **Enemy Forces**: The HVT is protected by a well-trained and well-equipped security detail, comprising approximately 20 personnel.
* **Time**: The operation will take place during the night, with a window of 3 hours to complete the mission.

### Objectives

* **Primary Objective**: Neutralize the HVT using a precision strike.
* **Secondary Objectives**:
	+ Minimize collateral damage to surrounding infrastructure and civilian populations.
	+ Gather intelligence on the HVT's security detail and any potential escape routes.

## Key Terms

* **HVT**: High-Value Target
* **Precision Strike**: A targeted attack using advanced munitions and/or specialized forces.
* **Reconnaissance**: The gathering of information about the enemy's dispositions, movements, and intentions.
* **Infiltration**: The act of inserting personnel or equipment into a hostile environment without being detected.
* **Exfiltration**: The act of extracting personnel or equipment from a hostile environment without being detected.

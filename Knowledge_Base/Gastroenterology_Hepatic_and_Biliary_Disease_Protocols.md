# Gastroenterology: Hepatic and Biliary Disease Protocols

## Overview

Hepatic and biliary disease protocols in gastroenterology involve the diagnosis, treatment, and management of various liver and bile duct disorders. These conditions can be acute or chronic, and may be caused by infections, toxins, metabolic disorders, or other factors.

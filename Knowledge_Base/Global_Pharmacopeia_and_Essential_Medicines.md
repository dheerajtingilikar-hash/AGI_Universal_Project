# Global Pharmacopeia and Essential Medicines

## Overview

The Global Pharmacopeia and Essential Medicines refers to a set of standardized medicines and pharmaceutical products that are recognized as essential for the treatment of priority diseases and conditions. The concept is based on the World Health Organization's (WHO) Essential Medicines List (EML), which is updated periodically to reflect the latest scientific evidence and global health needs.

The Global Pharmacopeia and Essential Medicines aims to ensure that all people have access to safe, effective, and affordable medicines, regardless of their geographical location or socio-economic status. This is achieved through the development of national pharmacopeias, which are compendia of standardized medicines that meet the quality and safety standards of the WHO.

### Definition

* [[Pharmacopeia]]: A collection of standardized medicines and pharmaceutical products that meet the quality and safety standards of a particular country or region.
* [[Essential Medicines]]: Medicines that are recognized as essential for the treatment of priority diseases and conditions, based on the latest scientific evidence and global health needs.
* [[World Health Organization (WHO)]]: The global authority on international health, responsible for setting standards and guidelines for the development and use of medicines.

### Key Concepts

* [[Quality and Safety Standards]]: The standards that ensure the quality and safety of medicines, including good manufacturing practices (GMP) and good laboratory practices (GLP).
* [[Access to Medicines]]: The ability of people to access safe, effective, and affordable medicines, regardless of their geographical location or socio-economic status.
* [[Global Health]]: The health of populations worldwide, including the prevention and treatment of diseases and conditions.

### Related Topics

* [[Pharmacovigilance]]: The science and activities related to the detection, assessment, understanding, and prevention of adverse effects or any other possible drug-related problems.
* [[Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)]]: The guidelines that ensure the quality and safety of medicines, including the design, construction, and operation of manufacturing facilities.
* [[Good Laboratory Practices (GLP)]]: The guidelines that ensure the quality and safety of medicines, including the design, conduct, and reporting of laboratory studies.

# Nihilism
Nihilism is a philosophical doctrine that suggests [[Life]] has no inherent [[Meaning]] or [[Value]].

## Overview
Nihilism is often associated with the rejection of traditional [[Morality]] and [[Ethics]]. It argues that [[Truth]] and [[Knowledge]] are unattainable, and that [[Reality]] is inherently [[Chaotic]] and [[Indeterminate]]. Nihilism can be further divided into several subcategories, including [[Moral Nihilism]], [[Epistemological Nihilism]], and [[Existential Nihilism]].

## Concept Map
* [[Nihilism]] 
	+ [[Moral Nihilism]]: rejection of traditional [[Morality]] and [[Ethics]]
	+ [[Epistemological Nihilism]]: rejection of the possibility of [[Knowledge]] and [[Truth]]
	+ [[Existential Nihilism]]: rejection of inherent [[Meaning]] and [[Value]] in [[Life]]
* [[Influence]]
	+ [[Friedrich Nietzsche]]: influential philosopher who wrote extensively on [[Nihilism]]
	+ [[Arthur Schopenhauer]]: philosopher who influenced the development of [[Nihilism]]
* [[Related Concepts]]
	+ [[Existentialism]]: philosophical doctrine that emphasizes individual [[Freedom]] and [[Choice]]
	+ [[Absurdism]]: philosophical doctrine that suggests the [[Absurd]] nature of [[Human Existence]]

# Maxwell’s Equations
Maxwell's Equations are a set of four fundamental equations in [[Classical Electromagnetism]] that describe the behavior of the [[Electric Field]] and [[Magnetic Field]].

## Overview
Maxwell's Equations are a formulation of the [[Laws of Physics]] that govern the behavior of [[Electromagnetic Radiation]]. They were formulated by [[James Clerk Maxwell]] and are a cornerstone of [[Classical Physics]]. The equations are:

1. [[Gauss's Law]] for electric fields: ∇⋅E = ρ/ε₀
2. [[Gauss's Law]] for magnetic fields: ∇⋅B = 0
3. [[Faraday's Law]] of induction: ∇×E = -∂B/∂t
4. [[Ampere's Law]] with Maxwell's correction: ∇×B = μ₀J + μ₀ε₀∂E/∂t

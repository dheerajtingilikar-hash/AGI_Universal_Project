# Botany
Botany is the scientific study of [[Plants]] and their interactions with the environment. It encompasses a wide range of topics, including [[Plant Anatomy]], [[Plant Physiology]], and [[Ecology]].

## Overview
Botany is a vital field of study, as plants are the primary producers of the Earth's [[Ecosystems]]. They play a crucial role in the [[Food Chain]], providing [[Nutrition]] for [[Herbivores]] and, in turn, [[Carnivores]]. Botany has numerous applications in [[Agriculture]], [[Horticulture]], and [[Conservation Biology]].

## Concept Map
The field of botany can be divided into several key areas, including:
* [[Plant Systematics]]: the study of [[Plant Classification]] and [[Phylogeny]]
* [[Plant Morphology]]: the study of [[Plant Structure]] and [[Development]]
* [[Plant Physiology]]: the study of [[Plant Function]] and [[Metabolism]]
* [[Ecology]]: the study of the interactions between plants and their environment
* [[Ethnobotany]]: the study of the relationships between plants and human cultures

Key concepts in botany include:
* [[Photosynthesis]]: the process by which plants produce [[Energy]] from [[Light]]
* [[Transpiration]]: the process by which plants transport [[Water]] and [[Minerals]] from the roots to the leaves
* [[Tropism]]: the response of plants to environmental stimuli, such as [[Light]] and [[Gravity]]

Related fields of study include:
* [[Myology]]: the study of [[Fungi]]
* [[Microbiology]]: the study of [[Microorganisms]]
* [[Zoology]]: the study of [[Animals]] and their interactions with plants.

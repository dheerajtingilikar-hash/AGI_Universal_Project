# Arctic Sovereignty

## Overview

Arctic sovereignty refers to the concept of territorial control and jurisdiction over the Arctic region, encompassing the eight Arctic states: Canada, Denmark (via Greenland), Finland, Iceland, Norway, Russia, Sweden, and the United States (via Alaska). The Arctic region is a unique and rapidly changing environment, with implications for global climate, security, and economic interests.

### Definition

Arctic sovereignty is a complex and multifaceted concept, encompassing:

- Territorial claims and boundaries
- Jurisdiction over natural resources (e.g., oil, gas, fisheries)
- Environmental protection and conservation
- Indigenous rights and self-governance
- International cooperation and governance

### Key Issues

- Territorial disputes and border conflicts
- Resource extraction and management
- Climate change and its impacts on Arctic ecosystems
- Indigenous rights and self-determination
- International governance and cooperation

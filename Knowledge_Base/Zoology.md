# Zoology
Zoology is the branch of [[Biology]] that focuses on the study of [[Animals]], including their structure, behavior, physiology, development, and evolution.

## Overview
Zoology is a diverse field that encompasses various subdisciplines, including [[Mammalogy]], [[Ornithology]], [[Herpetology]], [[Ichthyology]], and [[Entomology]]. Zoologists use a range of techniques, from laboratory experiments to field observations, to understand the complex interactions between animals and their environments. The study of zoology has numerous applications, including [[Conservation Biology]], [[Wildlife Management]], and [[Animal Behavior]].

## Subfields
* [[Animal Physiology]]: study of animal function and physiology
* [[Animal Development]]: study of animal development and growth
* [[Animal Evolution]]: study of animal evolution and phylogeny

## Applications
* [[Conservation Biology]]: application of zoological principles to conserve and manage [[Ecosystems]]
* [[Wildlife Management]]: application of zoological principles to manage and conserve [[Wildlife Populations]]

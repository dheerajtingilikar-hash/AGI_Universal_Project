# Evidence-Based Internal Medicine

## Overview

Evidence-Based Internal Medicine (EBIM) is an approach to medical practice that emphasizes the use of current best evidence in making decisions about patient care. It involves the integration of clinical expertise with the best available research evidence and patient values to guide medical decisions.

### Definition

EBIM is based on the principles of evidence-based medicine (EBM), which was first introduced by David Sackett in 1992. EBM is a systematic approach to medical decision-making that involves the following steps:

1. Formulating a clear question
2. Searching for the best available evidence
3. Evaluating the quality of the evidence
4. Applying the evidence to patient care

# Chemical Engineering
Chemical engineering is the branch of engineering that deals with the application of [[Physical Science]] and [[Mathematics]] to solve problems related to the production, transformation, and transportation of [[Chemicals]], [[Materials]], and [[Energy]].

## Overview
Chemical engineering involves the development, design, and operation of [[Process Systems]] that convert raw materials into valuable products. It encompasses various fields, including [[Thermodynamics]], [[Kinetics]], and [[Transport Phenomena]]. Chemical engineers use [[Computer Simulations]] and [[Experimental Methods]] to analyze and optimize [[Chemical Processes]], ensuring efficient, safe, and environmentally friendly operations.

## Concept Map
* [[Chemical Reaction Engineering]]: deals with the design and operation of [[Reactors]] and [[Separation Processes]]
* [[Process Control]]: involves the development of [[Control Systems]] to regulate and optimize [[Process Variables]]
* [[Thermodynamics]]: applies the principles of [[Energy]] and [[Entropy]] to analyze and design [[Energy Systems]]
* [[Mass Transfer]]: studies the transport of [[Mass]] and [[Momentum]] in [[Fluids]] and [[Solids]]
* [[Materials Science]]: examines the properties and applications of various [[Materials]] in chemical engineering processes

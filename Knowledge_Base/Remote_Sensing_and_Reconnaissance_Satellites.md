# Remote Sensing and Reconnaissance Satellites

## Overview

Remote sensing and reconnaissance satellites are a type of Earth observation satellite designed to collect data about the Earth's surface, atmosphere, and oceans. These satellites use various sensors and instruments to gather information about the environment, climate, weather, and human activities.

### Types of Remote Sensing Satellites

* **Optical Satellites**: Use visible, infrared, and ultraviolet light to capture images of the Earth's surface.
* **Radar Satellites**: Use radio waves to penetrate clouds and vegetation, providing information about the Earth's surface and topography.
* **Lidar Satellites**: Use laser light to measure the distance and properties of objects on the Earth's surface.
* **SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) Satellites**: Use radar pulses to create high-resolution images of the Earth's surface.

### Applications of Remote Sensing Satellites

* **Environmental Monitoring**: Track changes in land use, deforestation, and climate change.
* **Natural Disaster Response**: Monitor and predict natural disasters such as hurricanes, floods, and wildfires.
* **Agricultural Monitoring**: Track crop health, yield, and soil moisture.
* **Urban Planning**: Monitor urban growth, population density, and infrastructure development.

### Key Concepts

* **Resolution**: The level of detail in a satellite image.
* **Spectral Resolution**: The ability of a satellite sensor to distinguish between different wavelengths of light.
* **Spatial Resolution**: The ability of a satellite sensor to distinguish between different locations on the Earth's surface.
* **Temporal Resolution**: The frequency at which a satellite sensor collects data.

### Notable Satellites

* **Landsat**: A series of optical satellites launched by NASA to collect data about the Earth's surface.
* **MODIS (Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer)**: An instrument on NASA's Terra and Aqua satellites that collects data about the Earth's surface and atmosphere.
* **SARAL (Satellite with ARgos and ALtimeter)**: A radar satellite launched by ISRO to collect data about the Earth's surface and oceans.

# Topology
Topology is the branch of [[Mathematics]] that studies the properties of shapes and spaces that are preserved under continuous deformations, such as stretching and bending.

## Overview
Topology is a fundamental area of study in [[Mathematics]], focusing on the properties of shapes and spaces that remain invariant under continuous transformations. It has numerous applications in [[Physics]], [[Computer Science]], and [[Engineering]]. Topology is divided into several subfields, including [[Point-Set Topology]], [[Algebraic Topology]], and [[Differential Topology]].

## Concept Map
The key concepts in topology include:
* [[Metric Space]]: a set with a defined distance function
* [[Topological Space]]: a set with a defined topology
* [[Homeomorphism]]: a continuous transformation between two topological spaces
* [[Homotopy]]: a continuous deformation between two curves or surfaces
* [[Manifold]]: a topological space that is locally [[Euclidean Space]]
* [[Betti Number]]: a topological invariant that describes the number of holes in a space
* [[Euler Characteristic]]: a topological invariant that describes the overall shape of a space

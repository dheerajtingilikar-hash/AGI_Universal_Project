# Mechatronics
Mechatronics is an interdisciplinary field of engineering that combines principles from [[Mechanical Engineering]], [[Electrical Engineering]], and [[Computer Science]] to design, build, and operate intelligent systems.

## Overview
Mechatronics involves the integration of [[Electronics]], [[Mechanics]], and [[Software Engineering]] to create complex systems that can interact with their environment. Mechatronic systems are used in a wide range of applications, including [[Robotics]], [[Automotive Systems]], and [[Medical Devices]]. The field of mechatronics has evolved significantly over the years, with advancements in [[Artificial Intelligence]], [[Internet of Things (IoT)]], and [[Data Analytics]].

## Concept Map
The concept map of mechatronics includes the following key components:
* [[Sensors]]: devices that detect and measure physical parameters
* [[Actuators]]: devices that convert energy into motion or action
* [[Microcontrollers]]: small computers that control and coordinate system operations
* [[Software]]: programs that execute specific tasks and functions
* [[Communication Protocols]]: standards that enable data exchange between devices
* [[System Integration]]: the process of combining individual components into a cohesive system

## Applications
Mechatronic systems have numerous applications in various fields, including:
* [[Industrial Automation]]
* [[Aerospace Engineering]]
* [[Biomedical Engineering]]
* [[Consumer Electronics]]

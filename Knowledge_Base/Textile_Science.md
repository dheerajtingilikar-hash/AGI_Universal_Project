# Textile Science
Textile science is the study of the properties, production, and application of [[Textile]] materials. It encompasses various disciplines, including [[Materials Science]], [[Chemistry]], and [[Physics]], to understand the behavior and characteristics of textiles.

## Overview
Textile science involves the analysis of [[Fiber]] properties, [[Yarn]] structure, and [[Fabric]] formation. It also explores the effects of [[Finishing Treatment]]s, such as [[Dyeing]] and [[Printing]], on textile materials. The field of textile science is crucial for the development of new textile products, improvement of existing ones, and optimization of textile manufacturing processes.

## Concept Map
The concept map of textile science includes:
* [[Fiber Properties]]: [[Tensile Strength]], [[Elasticity]], and [[Moisture Absorption]]
* [[Yarn Structure]]: [[Twist]], [[Ply]], and [[Yarn Count]]
* [[Fabric Formation]]: [[Weaving]], [[Knitting]], and [[Nonwoven]] processes
* [[Finishing Treatments]]: [[Dyeing]], [[Printing]], and [[Finishing Chemical]]s
* [[Textile Testing]]: [[Tensile Testing]], [[Abrasion Resistance]], and [[Colorfastness]]

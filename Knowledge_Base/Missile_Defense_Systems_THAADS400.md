# Missile Defense Systems (THAAD/S-400)

## Overview

Missile defense systems are designed to detect, track, and intercept incoming ballistic missiles. Two prominent systems are the Terminal High Altitude Area Defense (THAAD) and the S-400 Triumf.

### THAAD

- **[[THAAD]]**: A United States Army missile defense system developed by Lockheed Martin.
- **[[Lockheed Martin]]**: The primary contractor for the THAAD system.
- **[[Terminal High Altitude Area Defense]]**: A ground-based missile defense system capable of intercepting ballistic missiles at high altitudes.

### S-400 Triumf

- **[[S-400 Triumf]]**: A Russian surface-to-air missile defense system developed by Almaz-Antey.
- **[[Almaz-Antey]]**: The primary contractor for the S-400 system.
- **[[Surface-to-air missile]]**: A type of missile designed to intercept and destroy aircraft and missiles.

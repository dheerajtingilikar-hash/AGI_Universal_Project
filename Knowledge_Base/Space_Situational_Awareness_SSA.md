# Space Situational Awareness (SSA)

## Overview

Space Situational Awareness (SSA) refers to the ability to collect, analyze, and disseminate information about the objects in Earth's orbit and beyond. This includes tracking and monitoring artificial satellites, space debris, asteroids, and other celestial bodies.

### Definition

SSA is a critical component of space operations, providing critical information for:

* **Collision avoidance**: Identifying potential collisions between satellites and other space objects
* **Satellite operations**: Optimizing satellite performance and lifespan
* **Space traffic management**: Regulating the use of space and preventing congestion
* **National security**: Enhancing situational awareness for military and defense purposes

### History

SSA has its roots in the 1960s, when the United States and the Soviet Union began tracking their respective satellite fleets. The concept gained momentum in the 1990s, with the establishment of the United States Space Surveillance Network (SSN) and the European Space Agency's (ESA) Space Situational Awareness programme.

### Key Concepts

* **Space Object**: Any object in Earth's orbit or beyond, including satellites, space debris, asteroids, and comets
* **Satellite**: An artificial object in orbit around Earth or another celestial body
* **Space Debris**: Natural or artificial objects in Earth's orbit, including fragments of satellites and other space objects
* **Tracking**: The process of determining the position and orbit of a space object
* **Monitoring**: The process of observing and analyzing the behavior of a space object

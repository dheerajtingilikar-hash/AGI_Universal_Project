# Molecular Assemblers

## Overview

Molecular assemblers are hypothetical devices capable of manipulating and assembling molecular structures at the atomic level. This concept is rooted in the fields of nanotechnology and molecular engineering.

### Definition

Molecular assemblers are envisioned as machines that can select and arrange individual atoms or molecules to form complex structures, such as proteins, DNA, or other biomolecules. This process would enable the creation of novel materials, pharmaceuticals, and other products with tailored properties.

### History

The concept of molecular assemblers has been explored in scientific literature since the 1960s, with notable contributions from scientists such as Richard Feynman and Eric Drexler.

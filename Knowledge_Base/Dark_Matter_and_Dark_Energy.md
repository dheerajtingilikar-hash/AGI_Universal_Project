# Dark Matter and Dark Energy
## Overview
Dark matter and dark energy are two mysterious components that make up approximately 95% of the universe. [[Dark Matter]] is a type of matter that does not emit, absorb, or reflect any electromagnetic radiation, making it invisible to our telescopes. [[Dark Energy]], on the other hand, is a type of energy that is thought to be responsible for the accelerating expansion of the universe.

## Concept Map
* [[Cosmology]]: the study of the origin, evolution, and fate of the universe
	+ [[Dark Matter]]: a type of matter that does not interact with light
		- [[Galactic Rotation Curves]]: the rotation curves of galaxies are flat, indicating the presence of dark matter
		- [[Galaxy Clusters]]: the distribution of galaxy clusters is influenced by dark matter
	+ [[Dark Energy]]: a type of energy that drives the accelerating expansion of the universe
		- [[Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation]]: the CMBR is affected by dark energy
		- [[Supernovae Observations]]: the observation of type Ia supernovae has led to the discovery of dark energy
* [[Theories of Gravity]]: [[General Relativity]] and [[Modified Newtonian Dynamics]] (MOND) are two theories that attempt to explain the behavior of dark matter and dark energy

## Properties of Dark Matter and Dark Energy
* [[Dark Matter]]: makes up approximately 27% of the universe's mass-energy density
* [[Dark Energy]]: makes up approximately 68% of the universe's mass-energy density
* [[Equation of State]]: the equation of state for dark energy is thought to be close to -1, indicating that it is a type of negative pressure

## Observational Evidence
* [[Galactic Rotation Curves]]: the rotation curves of galaxies are flat, indicating the presence of dark matter
* [[Galaxy Clusters]]: the distribution of galaxy clusters is influenced by dark matter
* [[Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation]]: the CMBR is affected by dark energy
* [[Supernovae Observations]]: the observation of type Ia supernovae has led to the discovery of dark energy

## Theoretical Frameworks
* [[Lambda-CDM Model]]: a model that describes the universe as being composed of approximately 68% dark energy, 27% dark matter, and 5% ordinary matter
* [[Modified Gravity Theories]]: theories that attempt to explain the behavior of dark matter and dark energy without invoking new particles or fields.

# Cyber Warfare and Network-Centric Operations

## Overview

Cyber warfare and network-centric operations refer to the use of cyberspace as a domain of warfare, where nations, organizations, and individuals engage in conflict, competition, or cooperation through the use of computer networks and cyber capabilities. This concept has evolved significantly with the advent of the internet and the increasing reliance on networked systems for military, economic, and social operations.

### Definition

Cyber warfare involves the use of cyber attacks, such as hacking, malware, and denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, to disrupt or destroy an adversary's computer systems, networks, or infrastructure. Network-centric operations, on the other hand, refer to the use of networked systems to enable real-time information sharing, coordination, and decision-making among military units, allies, and other stakeholders.

# Armored Personnel Carriers (APC)

## Overview

Armored Personnel Carriers (APC) are armored vehicles designed to transport troops safely across the battlefield. They are typically lightly armored, wheeled or tracked, and provide protection against small arms fire and shrapnel.

### Characteristics

- **Purpose**: Transport troops in combat zones
- **Design**: Lightly armored, wheeled or tracked
- **Capacity**: Typically 2-12 personnel
- **Speed**: Varies depending on the model, but generally up to 60 km/h
- **Range**: Varies depending on the model, but generally up to 500 km

## Types of APCs

- **Light Armored Vehicle (LAV)**: A lightly armored, wheeled or tracked vehicle designed for rapid transportation of troops.
- **Infantry Mobility Vehicle (IMV)**: A lightly armored, wheeled vehicle designed for rapid transportation of troops.
- **Mine Resistant Ambush Protected Vehicle (MRAP)**: A heavily armored vehicle designed to protect against mines and ambushes.

## Examples of APCs

- **M113 APC**: A tracked, lightly armored vehicle used by the US military.
- **BTR-60 APC**: A wheeled, lightly armored vehicle used by the Soviet Union and its allies.
- **LAV III APC**: A wheeled, lightly armored vehicle used by the Canadian military.

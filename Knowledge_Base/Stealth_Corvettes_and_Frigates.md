# Stealth Corvettes and Frigates

## Overview

Stealth corvettes and frigates are a class of warships designed to evade detection by radar and other sensors. These vessels utilize advanced stealth technology to reduce their radar cross-section (RCS), making them difficult to detect and track.

### Characteristics

- **Size**: Stealth corvettes and frigates are typically smaller than conventional warships, with lengths ranging from 80 to 120 meters.
- **Speed**: They are designed for high-speed operations, with top speeds exceeding 30 knots.
- **Armament**: Stealth corvettes and frigates are equipped with advanced missile systems, including anti-ship and anti-air missiles.
- **Propulsion**: They are often powered by gas turbines or diesel-electric propulsion systems.

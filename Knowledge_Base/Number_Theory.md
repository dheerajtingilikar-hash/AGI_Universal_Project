# Number Theory
Number theory is a branch of [[Mathematics]] that deals with the properties and behavior of [[Integers]] and other whole numbers.

## Overview
Number theory is a vast and fascinating field that has been studied for thousands of years. It encompasses a wide range of topics, including [[Divisibility]], [[Prime Numbers]], [[Congruences]], and [[Diophantine Equations]]. Number theory has numerous applications in [[Computer Science]], [[Cryptography]], and [[Code Theory]].

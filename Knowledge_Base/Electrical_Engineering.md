# Electrical Engineering
Electrical Engineering is a field of [[Engineering]] that deals with the study and application of [[Electricity]], [[Electronics]], and [[Electromagnetism]]. It involves the design, development, and maintenance of electrical systems, including [[Electrical Circuits]], [[Electronic Devices]], and [[Power Systems]].

## Overview
Electrical Engineering is a broad field that encompasses various subfields, including [[Power Engineering]], [[Control Systems]], and [[Telecommunications]]. It has numerous applications in [[Energy Generation]], [[Transportation]], and [[Communication Systems]]. Electrical engineers use mathematical and computational tools, such as [[Circuit Analysis]] and [[Signal Processing]], to design and optimize electrical systems.

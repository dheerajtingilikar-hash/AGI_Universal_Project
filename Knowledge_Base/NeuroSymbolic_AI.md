# Neuro-Symbolic AI

## Overview

Neuro-Symbolic AI is a subfield of artificial intelligence that combines the strengths of symbolic and connectionist AI approaches. This approach aims to leverage the benefits of both paradigms to create more efficient, interpretable, and generalizable AI systems.

### Definition

Neuro-Symbolic AI is defined as a type of AI that integrates symbolic representations with neural networks to achieve more robust and flexible reasoning capabilities.

### Key Features

- **Hybrid Architecture**: Neuro-Symbolic AI systems typically employ a hybrid architecture that combines symbolic and connectionist components.
- **Symbolic Reasoning**: These systems use symbolic representations to reason about abstract concepts and relationships.
- **Neural Network**: Neural networks are used to learn and represent complex patterns in data.

# International Relations
International relations refer to the interactions and exchanges between different [[Sovereign State|sovereign states]] and other entities in the global arena.

## Overview
International relations involve a complex array of [[Diplomacy|diplomatic]], [[Economy|economic]], [[Culture|cultural]], and [[Security|security]] interactions between states and other international actors. The field of international relations encompasses various [[Subfield|subfields]], including [[International Security|international security]], [[International Political Economy|international political economy]], and [[International Organization|international organization]]. Key concepts in international relations include [[Sovereignty|sovereignty]], [[Non-Intervention|non-intervention]], and [[Self-Determination|self-determination]].

## Concept Map
The study of international relations can be organized around several key concepts and themes:
* [[Global Governance|Global governance]]: refers to the system of rules, norms, and institutions that regulate international interactions
* [[International Law|International law]]: provides a framework for understanding the rights and obligations of states and other international actors
* [[International Conflict|International conflict]]: includes [[War|war]], [[Terrorism|terrorism]], and other forms of [[Violence|violence]] that occur between states or other international actors
* [[International Cooperation|International cooperation]]: involves collaborative efforts between states and other international actors to address common challenges and achieve shared goals
* [[International System|International system]]: refers to the overall structure and dynamics of the international arena, including the interactions and relationships between states and other international actors.

# Electromagnetism
Electromagnetism is a fundamental physical phenomenon that describes the interaction between [[Electricity]] and [[Magnetism]]. It is a branch of [[Physics]] that deals with the study of the electromagnetic force, one of the four fundamental forces of nature.

## Overview
Electromagnetism is a unifying theory that explains the relationship between [[Electric Field|electric fields]] and [[Magnetic Field|magnetic fields]]. It was formulated by [[James Clerk Maxwell]], who introduced a set of equations, known as [[Maxwell's Equations]], that describe the behavior of the electromagnetic field. Electromagnetism has numerous applications in [[Technology]], including [[Electrical Engineering]], [[Telecommunications]], and [[Medical Imaging]].

## Concept Map
The concept of electromagnetism can be broken down into several key areas:
* [[Electromagnetic Radiation]]: includes [[Light]], [[Radio Waves]], and [[X-Rays]]
* [[Electromagnetic Induction]]: the process by which a changing [[Magnetic Field]] induces an [[Electric Field]]
* [[Electromagnetic Waves]]: a type of wave that propagates through the [[Electromagnetic Field]]
* [[Lorentz Force]]: the force exerted on a charged particle by an [[Electric Field]] and a [[Magnetic Field]]
* [[Gauss's Law]]: a fundamental principle that relates the distribution of [[Electric Charge]] to the resulting [[Electric Field]]

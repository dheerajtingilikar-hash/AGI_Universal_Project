# Sustainable Energy
Sustainable energy refers to the provision of [[Energy]] that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

## Overview
Sustainable energy is a critical component of [[Renewable Energy]] and [[Energy Efficiency]]. It encompasses various forms of energy production, including [[Solar Energy]], [[Wind Energy]], [[Hydro Energy]], and [[Geothermal Energy]]. The primary goal of sustainable energy is to reduce [[Greenhouse Gas Emissions]] and mitigate the impacts of [[Climate Change]].

## Concept Map
The concept of sustainable energy can be broken down into the following key components:
* [[Renewable Energy Sources]]: [[Solar Energy]], [[Wind Energy]], [[Hydro Energy]], and [[Geothermal Energy]]
* [[Energy Efficiency]]: [[Energy Storage]], [[Smart Grids]], and [[Green Buildings]]
* [[Sustainable Energy Policies]]: [[Carbon Pricing]], [[Renewable Portfolio Standards]], and [[Energy Subsidies]]
* [[Energy Access]]: [[Energy Poverty]], [[Energy Equity]], and [[Universal Energy Access]]

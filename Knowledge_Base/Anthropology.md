# Anthropology
Anthropology is the [[Science]] of studying [[Human]] beings, including their behavior, customs, and [[Biological]] characteristics, past and present.

## Overview
[[Anthropology]] is a [[Discipline]] that seeks to understand the [[Complexity]] of human experiences and [[Culture]]s. It is divided into four main [[Subfield]]s: 
- [[Sociocultural Anthropology]]
- [[Linguistic Anthropology]]
- [[Biological Anthropology]]
- [[Archaeology]]

## Concept Map
The study of [[Anthropology]] involves various key [[Concept]]s, including:
- [[Evolution]]: the process of change in [[Species]] over time
- [[Culture]]: the [[Social]] behaviors, [[Norm]]s, and [[Value]]s of a particular group
- [[Ethnography]]: the [[Methodology]] of describing and analyzing [[Culture]]s
- [[Ethnology]]: the comparative study of [[Culture]]s
- [[Primatology]]: the study of [[Primate]]s, including their behavior and [[Evolution]]ary history

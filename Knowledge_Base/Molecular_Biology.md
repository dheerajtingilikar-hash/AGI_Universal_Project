# Molecular Biology
Molecular biology is a branch of [[Biology]] that focuses on the study of [[Biomolecule]]s and their interactions within [[Cell (biology)|cells]].

## Overview
Molecular biology is an interdisciplinary field that combines principles from [[Chemistry]], [[Physics]], and [[Genetics]] to understand the molecular mechanisms underlying [[Biological process|biological processes]]. It involves the study of [[DNA]], [[RNA]], [[Protein]], and other biomolecules, as well as their roles in [[Cell signaling]], [[Gene expression]], and [[Metabolism]].

## Concept Map
The following concept map illustrates the relationships between key concepts in molecular biology:
- [[Molecular biology]]: study of biomolecules and their interactions
  - [[Genetics]]: study of [[Gene|genes]] and their inheritance
    - [[DNA]]: double-stranded [[Nucleic acid|nucleic acid]] containing genetic information
    - [[Gene expression]]: process by which genetic information is converted into [[Protein]]
  - [[Biochemistry]]: study of [[Chemical reaction|chemical reactions]] within cells
    - [[Metabolism]]: set of chemical reactions involved in energy production and [[Biomolecule|biomolecule]] synthesis
    - [[Cell signaling]]: process by which cells communicate with each other through [[Signal transduction|signal transduction]] pathways
  - [[Molecular genetics]]: study of the structure and function of [[Gene|genes]] at the molecular level
    - [[Genetic engineering]]: use of [[Biotechnology|biotechnology]] to manipulate [[Gene|genes]] and [[Genome|genomes]]

# Ornithology and Avian Migration

## Overview

Ornithology is the scientific study of birds, encompassing their behavior, physiology, evolution, classification, and distribution. Avian migration refers to the seasonal movement of birds between their breeding and wintering grounds. This phenomenon is a crucial aspect of ornithology, with millions of birds undertaking long-distance journeys each year.

### Definition

Avian migration is defined as the periodic movement of birds between their breeding and wintering grounds, often in response to changes in food availability, temperature, and daylight hours.

### Types of Migration

- **Altitudinal migration**: movement between high-altitude breeding grounds and low-altitude wintering grounds.
- **Altitudinal-altitudinal migration**: movement between high-altitude breeding grounds and high-altitude wintering grounds.
- **Latitude-altitude migration**: movement between high-latitude breeding grounds and low-latitude wintering grounds.
- **Latitude-latitude migration**: movement between high-latitude breeding grounds and high-latitude wintering grounds.

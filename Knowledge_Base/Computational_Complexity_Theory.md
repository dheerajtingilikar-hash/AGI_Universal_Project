# Computational Complexity Theory
Computational Complexity Theory is a branch of [[Computer Science]] that focuses on the study of the resources required to solve computational problems. It is concerned with the classification of problems into different complexity classes based on their computational resources, such as [[Time Complexity]] and [[Space Complexity]].

## Overview
Computational Complexity Theory provides a framework for understanding the inherent difficulty of computational problems. It helps to identify the problems that can be solved efficiently and those that are likely to be intractable. The theory has numerous applications in [[Cryptography]], [[Algorithm Design]], and [[Optimization Problems]].

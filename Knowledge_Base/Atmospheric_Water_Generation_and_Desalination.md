# Atmospheric Water Generation and Desalination

## Overview

Atmospheric water generation and desalination are technologies used to extract water from the atmosphere and seawater, respectively. These methods are crucial for providing clean drinking water in areas where traditional sources are scarce or contaminated.

### Definition

- **Atmospheric Water Generation (AWG)**: A process that extracts water from the air using condensation, evaporation, or other methods.
- **Desalination**: A process that removes salt and other minerals from seawater or brackish water to produce freshwater.

### Types of AWG

- **Condensation-based AWG**: Uses condensation to extract water from the air.
- **Evaporation-based AWG**: Uses evaporation to extract water from the air.
- **Membrane-based AWG**: Uses semipermeable membranes to extract water from the air.

### Types of Desalination

- **Reverse Osmosis (RO)**: Uses semipermeable membranes to remove salt and other minerals from seawater.
- **Multi-Stage Flash Distillation (MSF)**: Uses heat to evaporate seawater and then condense the vapor to produce freshwater.
- **Multi-Effect Distillation (MED)**: Uses heat to evaporate seawater and then condense the vapor to produce freshwater.

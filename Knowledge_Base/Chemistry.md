# Chemistry
Chemistry is the scientific study of the composition, properties, and reactions of [[Matter]]. It involves the analysis of the structure, properties, and transformations of [[Substance]]s.

## Overview
Chemistry is a vast and diverse field that encompasses various branches, including [[Organic Chemistry]], [[Inorganic Chemistry]], [[Physical Chemistry]], and [[Biochemistry]]. It is closely related to other scientific disciplines, such as [[Physics]] and [[Biology]]. Chemistry plays a crucial role in understanding the natural world and has numerous applications in fields like [[Medicine]], [[Energy]], and [[Environmental Science]].

## Concept Map
The concept of chemistry can be broken down into several key areas:
* [[Atomic Structure]]: The study of the structure and properties of [[Atom]]s.
* [[Chemical Bonding]]: The study of the forces that hold [[Molecule]]s together.
* [[Chemical Reactions]]: The study of the transformations that occur when [[Substance]]s interact.
* [[Thermodynamics]]: The study of the relationships between [[Energy]], [[Temperature]], and [[Work]].
* [[Kinetics]]: The study of the rates of [[Chemical Reaction]]s.

# Small Arms Engineering and Ballistics

## Overview

Small arms engineering and ballistics is a multidisciplinary field that combines mechanical engineering, materials science, and physics to design, develop, and test firearms and other small arms. The field encompasses the study of the mechanical and aerodynamic properties of projectiles, as well as the behavior of firearms under various operating conditions.

## Subtopics

### Projectile Dynamics

- [[Aerodynamics]]: The study of the interaction between projectiles and air.
- [[Trajectory]]: The path of a projectile in flight.
- [[Terminal Ballistics]]: The study of the behavior of projectiles upon impact.

### Ballistics

- [[Exterior Ballistics]]: The study of the motion of projectiles in the atmosphere.
- [[Interior Ballistics]]: The study of the motion of projectiles within a firearm.
- [[Terminal Ballistics]]: The study of the behavior of projectiles upon impact.

### Firearm Design

- [[Action Types]]: The different types of firearm actions, such as bolt-action and semi-automatic.
- [[Barrel Design]]: The design and construction of firearm barrels.
- [[Stock Design]]: The design and construction of firearm stocks.

### Materials Science

- [[Materials Properties]]: The physical and chemical properties of materials used in firearms.
- [[Material Selection]]: The selection of materials for use in firearms.

### Mechanical Engineering

- [[Mechanical Systems]]: The study of mechanical systems and their applications in firearms.
- [[Kinematics]]: The study of the motion of objects without considering the forces that cause the motion.
- [[Dynamics]]: The study of the motion of objects and the forces that cause the motion.

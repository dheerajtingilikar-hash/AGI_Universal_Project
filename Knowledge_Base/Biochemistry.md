# Biochemistry
Biochemistry is the study of the [[chemical]] processes that occur within living [[organisms]]. It is a multidisciplinary field that combines principles from [[chemistry]], [[biology]], and [[physics]] to understand the complex interactions between [[biomolecules]].

## Overview
Biochemistry involves the study of the structure, function, and interactions of [[biomolecules]] such as [[proteins]], [[carbohydrates]], [[lipids]], and [[nucleic acids]]. It also explores the metabolic [[pathways]] that occur within cells, including [[glycolysis]], [[citric acid cycle]], and [[oxidative phosphorylation]]. Understanding biochemistry is essential for understanding how [[cells]] function, how [[organisms]] respond to their environment, and how [[diseases]] can be treated.

## Concept Map
The following concept map illustrates the key areas of study in biochemistry:
- [[Biomolecules]]: [[proteins]], [[carbohydrates]], [[lipids]], and [[nucleic acids]]
- [[Metabolic pathways]]: [[glycolysis]], [[citric acid cycle]], and [[oxidative phosphorylation]]
- [[Cell signaling]]: [[signal transduction]] and [[gene expression]]
- [[Genetics]]: [[DNA replication]], [[transcription]], and [[translation]]
- [[Diseases]]: [[genetic disorders]], [[infectious diseases]], and [[cancer]]

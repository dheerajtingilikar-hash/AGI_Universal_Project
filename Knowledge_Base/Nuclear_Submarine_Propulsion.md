# Nuclear Submarine Propulsion

## Overview

Nuclear submarine propulsion is a type of propulsion system used in nuclear-powered submarines. It utilizes a nuclear reactor to generate steam, which drives a turbine connected to a propeller or a pump-jet. This system provides a high power-to-weight ratio, allowing nuclear submarines to operate for extended periods at high speeds.

### Advantages

- High power output
- Long endurance
- Low noise signature
- High speed capabilities

### Disadvantages

- High initial cost
- Complexity of nuclear reactor maintenance
- Radioactive waste management

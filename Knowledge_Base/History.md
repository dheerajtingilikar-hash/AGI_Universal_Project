# History
History refers to the study of past events, particularly human affairs. It is a broad field that encompasses various disciplines, including [[Archaeology]], [[Anthropology]], and [[Sociology]].

## Overview
The study of history involves the collection, analysis, and interpretation of historical data, which can be derived from various sources, including [[Primary Sources]] and [[Secondary Sources]]. Historians use these sources to reconstruct the past, understand the causes and consequences of historical events, and identify patterns and trends that have shaped human societies.

## Concept Map
The concept of history is closely related to other fields, including:
* [[Philosophy of History]], which explores the nature and meaning of historical events
* [[Historiography]], which examines the methods and principles of historical research and writing
* [[Cultural Heritage]], which refers to the preservation and transmission of cultural traditions and historical artifacts
* [[Time]], which is a fundamental concept in understanding historical events and processes

Key historical periods and events include:
* [[Ancient Civilizations]], such as [[Egypt]] and [[Greece]]
* [[Medieval Period]], which saw the rise of [[Feudalism]] and the [[Catholic Church]]
* [[Industrial Revolution]], which transformed the global economy and society
* [[World War I]] and [[World War II]], which had a profound impact on international relations and global politics

Historical figures, such as [[Napoleon Bonaparte]] and [[Albert Einstein]], have also played a significant role in shaping the course of human history.

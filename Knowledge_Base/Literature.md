# Literature
Literature refers to the body of written works, including [[Novel]]s, [[Poetry]], [[Drama]], and other forms of creative writing.

## Overview
Literature is a broad term that encompasses a wide range of genres, styles, and periods. It includes works of [[Fiction]], [[Nonfiction]], and [[Hybrid]] forms, and can be classified into various categories such as [[Prose]], [[Verse]], and [[Drama]]. Literature serves as a means of expression, communication, and reflection of human experiences, thoughts, and emotions.

## Concept Map
The concept of literature can be mapped to various related terms and ideas, including:
* [[Language]]: the medium through which literature is created and conveyed
* [[Author]]: the creator of a literary work
* [[Genre]]: a category of literature based on style, form, or content
* [[Theme]]: a central idea or message in a literary work
* [[Symbolism]]: the use of symbols, motifs, and other literary devices to convey meaning
* [[Literary Theory]]: the study of the principles and methods of literary analysis and interpretation
* [[Literary Movement]]: a group of writers and works characterized by a shared style, ideology, or historical context

## Subfields
Literature can be divided into several subfields, including:
* [[Comparative Literature]]: the study of literary works across different cultures and languages
* [[Literary History]]: the study of the development of literature over time
* [[Literary Criticism]]: the analysis and interpretation of literary works
* [[Creative Writing]]: the practice of writing original literary works

## Related Fields
Literature is closely related to other fields of study, including:
* [[Linguistics]]: the study of language structure, syntax, and semantics
* [[Philosophy]]: the study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, and reality
* [[History]]: the study of past events, cultures, and societies
* [[Cultural Studies]]: the study of cultural practices, values, and beliefs.

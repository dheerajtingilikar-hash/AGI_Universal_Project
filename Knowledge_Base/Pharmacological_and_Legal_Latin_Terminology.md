# Pharmacological and Legal Latin Terminology

## Overview

Pharmacological and legal Latin terminology refers to the use of Latin words and phrases in the fields of pharmacology and law. This terminology is often used to create precise and unambiguous terms for medications, diseases, and legal concepts.

### History

Latin has been used in medicine and law for centuries, dating back to the Roman Empire. The use of Latin in these fields continued through the Middle Ages and into the modern era.

### Importance

Latin terminology is important in pharmacology and law because it provides a universal language that can be understood by professionals across different countries and cultures. This helps to ensure consistency and accuracy in communication.

* [[Mens Rea]]: a guilty mind or intent
* [[Actus Reus]]: a guilty act
* [[Causa Mortis]]: a cause of death

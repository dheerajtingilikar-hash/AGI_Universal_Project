# Fashion Illustration
Fashion illustration is an art form that combines [[Fashion]] and [[Illustration]] to communicate fashion ideas and designs.

## Overview
Fashion illustration is a visual representation of [[Clothing]] and [[Textiles]] used to convey the aesthetic and style of a particular fashion brand, designer, or trend. It involves creating detailed and stylized drawings or paintings of garments, accessories, and models to showcase the latest fashion designs. Fashion illustrators use a range of techniques, including [[Watercolor]], [[Ink]], and [[Digital Art]], to create their artwork.

## Concept Map
The concept of fashion illustration can be broken down into several key areas:
* [[Fashion Design]]: the process of creating original clothing and accessory designs
* [[Fashion Drawing]]: the technique of drawing and rendering fashion designs
* [[Fashion Art]]: the creative expression of fashion through various art forms, including illustration, photography, and sculpture
* [[Fashion Media]]: the channels through which fashion illustration is communicated, including [[Magazines]], [[Websites]], and [[Social Media]]
* [[Fashion Industry]]: the business and commercial aspects of the fashion world, including [[Fashion Brands]], [[Designers]], and [[Models]]

## History
Fashion illustration has a long history, dating back to the 17th and 18th centuries when [[Fashion Plates]] were used to showcase the latest fashion trends. Over time, fashion illustration has evolved to incorporate new techniques and technologies, including [[Computer-Aided Design]] and [[Digital Illustration]].

## Notable Fashion Illustrators
Some notable fashion illustrators include [[Christian Dior]], [[Coco Chanel]], and [[Andy Warhol]], who have all made significant contributions to the field of fashion illustration.

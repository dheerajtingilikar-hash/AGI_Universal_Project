# Organic Chemistry
Organic chemistry is the branch of [[Chemistry]] that deals with the study of [[Carbon]]-based compounds and their properties, reactions, and synthesis.

## Overview
Organic chemistry involves the study of [[Molecule]]s that contain [[Carbon]]-[[Hydrogen]] bonds, as well as other elements such as [[Oxygen]], [[Nitrogen]], and [[Sulfur]]. It encompasses a wide range of topics, including the structure and properties of [[Alkane]]s, [[Alkene]]s, [[Alkyne]]s, and [[Aromatic Compound]]s. Organic chemistry also involves the study of [[Reaction Mechanism]]s, including [[Substitution Reaction]]s, [[Elimination Reaction]]s, and [[Addition Reaction]]s.

## Concept Map
* [[Functional Group]]s: [[Alcohol]]s, [[Ester]]s, [[Ether]]s, [[Aldehyde]]s, and [[Ketone]]s
* [[Reaction Type]]s: [[Nucleophilic Substitution]], [[Electrophilic Addition]], and [[Radical Reaction]]s
* [[Spectroscopy]]: [[Infrared Spectroscopy]], [[Nuclear Magnetic Resonance]] (NMR), and [[Mass Spectrometry]]
* [[Synthetic Method]]s: [[Friedel-Crafts Reaction]], [[Grignard Reaction]], and [[Wittig Reaction]]
* [[Biological Compound]]s: [[Carbohydrate]]s, [[Lipid]]s, [[Protein]]s, and [[Nucleic Acid]]s

## Key Concepts
* [[Stereochemistry]]: the study of the three-dimensional arrangement of atoms in a molecule
* [[Acid-Base Chemistry]]: the study of [[Acid]]s and [[Base]]s in organic chemistry
* [[Organic Synthesis]]: the process of designing and synthesizing complex organic molecules

## Related Fields
* [[Biochemistry]]: the study of the chemical processes that occur within living organisms
* [[Medicinal Chemistry]]: the study of the design, synthesis, and development of [[Pharmaceutical]]s
* [[Materials Science]]: the study of the properties and applications of materials, including organic materials.

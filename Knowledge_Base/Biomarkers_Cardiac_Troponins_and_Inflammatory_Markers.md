# Biomarkers: Cardiac Troponins and Inflammatory Markers

## Overview

Biomarkers are measurable indicators of a biological process or a disease state. Cardiac troponins and inflammatory markers are two types of biomarkers used in clinical settings to diagnose and monitor cardiovascular diseases.

### Cardiac Troponins

Cardiac troponins are a family of proteins found in cardiac muscle cells. They play a crucial role in regulating muscle contraction. Elevated levels of cardiac troponins in the blood are indicative of cardiac damage, such as myocardial infarction (MI) or cardiac injury.

### Inflammatory Markers

Inflammatory markers are biomarkers that indicate the presence of inflammation in the body. They are often used to diagnose and monitor conditions such as acute coronary syndrome (ACS), sepsis, and chronic inflammatory diseases.

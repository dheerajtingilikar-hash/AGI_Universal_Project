# Organometallic Chemistry
Organometallic chemistry is a subfield of [[Chemistry]] that deals with the study of [[Chemical Compound|chemical compounds]] that contain a bond between a [[Metal]] and a [[Carbon]] atom.

## Overview
Organometallic chemistry combines aspects of [[Inorganic Chemistry]] and [[Organic Chemistry]] to study the properties, synthesis, and reactions of organometallic compounds. These compounds have a wide range of applications in [[Catalysis]], [[Materials Science]], and [[Pharmaceuticals]]. The field of organometallic chemistry has led to the development of many important [[Industrial Process|industrial processes]] and has played a key role in the discovery of new [[Chemical Reaction|chemical reactions]].

## Concept Map
The concept map of organometallic chemistry includes the following key topics:
* [[Coordination Chemistry]]: the study of [[Coordination Compound|coordination compounds]] and their properties
* [[Organometallic Compound|Organometallic compounds]]: the study of compounds that contain a bond between a metal and a carbon atom
* [[Transition Metal]]: the study of metals that can form ions with different [[Oxidation State|oxidation states]]
* [[Catalyst]]: the study of substances that can speed up [[Chemical Reaction|chemical reactions]] without being consumed by the reaction
* [[Organic Synthesis]]: the study of the synthesis of [[Organic Compound|organic compounds]] using organometallic compounds as catalysts or reagents.

## Key Reactions
Some key reactions in organometallic chemistry include:
* [[Hydrogenation Reaction|Hydrogenation reactions]]: the addition of [[Hydrogen]] to a molecule using an organometallic catalyst
* [[Cross-Coupling Reaction|Cross-coupling reactions]]: the formation of a new bond between two molecules using an organometallic catalyst
* [[Oxidative Addition|Oxidative addition reactions]]: the addition of a molecule to a metal center, resulting in the formation of a new bond and an increase in the oxidation state of the metal.

## Applications
Organometallic chemistry has a wide range of applications in fields such as:
* [[Catalysis]]: the use of organometallic compounds as catalysts to speed up chemical reactions
* [[Materials Science]]: the use of organometallic compounds to synthesize new materials with unique properties
* [[Pharmaceuticals]]: the use of organometallic compounds as reagents or catalysts in the synthesis of pharmaceuticals.

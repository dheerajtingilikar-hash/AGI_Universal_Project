# The Hero's Journey (Monomyth)
The Hero's Journey, also known as the Monomyth, is a concept developed by [[Joseph Campbell]] that describes the stages a hero goes through in a transformative journey.

## Overview
The Hero's Journey is a universal pattern that appears in stories from cultures around the world. It is a metaphor for the journey of self-discovery and transformation that individuals undergo in their lives. The journey consists of several stages, including the [[Call to Adventure]], [[Refusal of the Call]], [[Meeting the Mentor]], [[Crossing the Threshold]], [[Tests, Allies, and Enemies]], [[Approach to the Inmost Cave]], [[The Ordeal]], [[Reward (Seizing the Sword)]], [[The Road Back]], [[The Resurrection]], and [[Return with the Elixir]].

## Concept Map
The Hero's Journey can be represented as a concept map, with the following stages and connections:
- [[The Ordinary World]]: The hero's starting point, where they are introduced and their normal life is established.
- [[The Call to Adventure]]: The hero receives a call to embark on a journey, which disrupts their ordinary life.
- [[Refusal of the Call]]: The hero is initially reluctant to accept the call, due to fear or insecurity.
- [[Meeting the Mentor]]: The hero meets a guide or mentor who helps them prepare for the journey.
- [[Crossing the Threshold]]: The hero leaves their ordinary world and crosses into the special world of the journey.
- [[Tests, Allies, and Enemies]]: The hero encounters various challenges, meets allies, and faces enemies.
- [[Approach to the Inmost Cave]]: The hero approaches a critical moment of confrontation or a symbolic "inmost cave".
- [[The Ordeal]]: The hero faces a severe test or confrontation, often with a powerful enemy.
- [[Reward (Seizing the Sword)]]: The hero achieves a triumph or gain, which prepares them for the journey back.
- [[The Road Back]]: The hero begins their journey back to the ordinary world.
- [[The Resurrection]]: The hero faces a final test or confrontation, and is reborn or transformed.
- [[Return with the Elixir]]: The hero returns to the ordinary world, bringing with them the wisdom or power gained on their journey.

The Hero's Journey is a fundamental concept in [[Mythology]] and [[Storytelling]], and has been applied in various fields, including [[Psychology]], [[Literature]], and [[Film]].

# Metaphysics
Metaphysics is the branch of [[Philosophy]] that deals with the nature of [[Reality]], including the relationship between [[Mind]] and [[Matter]], [[Substance]] and [[Attribute]], and [[Potentiality]] and [[Actuality]].

## Overview
Metaphysics is concerned with exploring the fundamental nature of [[Existence]], [[Time]], and [[Space]]. It involves the study of [[Ontology]], which is the investigation of [[Being]] and what exists, as well as the relationships between different entities. Metaphysics also encompasses the study of [[Cosmology]], which is the examination of the origin and structure of the [[Universe]].

## Concept Map
The following concepts are central to the study of metaphysics:
- [[Ontology]]: the study of [[Being]] and what exists
- [[Epistemology]]: the study of [[Knowledge]] and how it is acquired
- [[Cosmology]]: the study of the origin and structure of the [[Universe]]
- [[Teleology]]: the study of [[Purpose]] and [[Final Cause]]
- [[Determinism]]: the idea that every event is the inevitable result of prior causes
- [[Free Will]]: the idea that individuals have the ability to make choices that are not determined by prior causes
- [[Dualism]]: the idea that [[Mind]] and [[Matter]] are separate entities
- [[Monism]]: the idea that [[Mind]] and [[Matter]] are ultimately the same entity

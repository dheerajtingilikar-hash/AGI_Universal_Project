# Neuroscience
Neuroscience is the [[Science]] of studying the [[Nervous System|nervous system]] and its functions.

## Overview
Neuroscience is an [[Interdisciplinary]] field that combines [[Biology]], [[Psychology]], [[Computer Science]], and [[Mathematics]] to understand the structure and function of the [[Brain]] and [[Nervous System]]. It encompasses various subfields, including [[Cognitive Neuroscience]], [[Neuroanatomy]], [[Neurophysiology]], and [[Neuropsychology]].

## Concept Map
The key concepts in neuroscience are:
* [[Neuron]]: the basic building block of the nervous system
* [[Synapse]]: the gap between two neurons where [[Neurotransmitter]]s are released
* [[Neuroplasticity]]: the brain's ability to reorganize itself in response to new experiences
* [[Sensory System]]: the system responsible for processing [[Sensory Information]] from the environment
* [[Motor System]]: the system responsible for controlling [[Movement]] and [[Action]]

Related fields:
* [[Psychology]]: the study of [[Behavior]] and [[Mental Processes]]
* [[Neurology]]: the study of [[Nervous System Disorders]] and their treatment
* [[Artificial Intelligence]]: the development of [[Intelligent Systems]] that mimic human cognition

Key techniques:
* [[Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging]] (fMRI): a method for measuring [[Brain Activity]]
* [[Electroencephalography]] (EEG): a method for measuring [[Electrical Activity]] in the brain
* [[Neurostimulation]]: a technique for modulating brain activity using [[Electrical]] or [[Magnetic]] stimulation.

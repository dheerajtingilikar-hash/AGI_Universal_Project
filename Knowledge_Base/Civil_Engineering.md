# Civil Engineering
Civil engineering is the [[Discipline]] of engineering that deals with the design, construction, and maintenance of physical infrastructure, such as [[Bridge]]s, [[Road]]s, [[Canal]]s, and [[Building]]s.

## Overview
Civil engineering is a broad field that encompasses various specialties, including [[Structural Engineering]], [[Transportation Engineering]], [[Water Resources Engineering]], and [[Geotechnical Engineering]]. Civil engineers apply scientific and mathematical principles to develop solutions to real-world problems, ensuring the safety, efficiency, and sustainability of infrastructure projects.

## Concept Map
* [[Civil Engineering]] 
	+ [[Structural Engineering]]: design and analysis of [[Building]]s, [[Bridge]]s, and other structures
	+ [[Transportation Engineering]]: planning, design, and operation of [[Road]]s, [[Highway]]s, and [[Public Transportation]] systems
	+ [[Water Resources Engineering]]: management of [[Water Supply]] systems, [[Irrigation]] systems, and [[Flood Control]] measures
	+ [[Geotechnical Engineering]]: analysis and design of [[Foundation]]s, [[Tunnel]]s, and other underground structures
* Related fields: [[Architecture]], [[Urban Planning]], [[Environmental Engineering]], and [[Construction Management]]

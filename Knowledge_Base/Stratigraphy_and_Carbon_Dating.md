# Stratigraphy and Carbon Dating
## Overview
Stratigraphy is the study of [[Rock Layers]] and their [[Geologic Time Scale|order]] in the [[Earth]]'s [[Crust]]. It provides a framework for understanding the [[Earth]]'s [[History]] and is closely related to [[Carbon Dating]], a technique used to determine the [[Age]] of [[Organic Materials]]. [[Carbon Dating]] is based on the [[Radioactive Decay]] of [[Carbon-14]] and is widely used in [[Archaeology]] and [[Geology]].

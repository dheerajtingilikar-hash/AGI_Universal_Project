# Computer Science
## Overview
Computer Science is the study of [[Algorithm]]s, [[Computer Hardware]], and [[Software]] that enables the efficient and effective use of [[Computer]]s. It encompasses a broad range of subfields, including [[Artificial Intelligence]], [[Computer Networks]], [[Database Systems]], and [[Human-Computer Interaction]]. Computer Science has numerous applications in various industries, such as [[Healthcare]], [[Finance]], and [[Education]].

## Subfields
Some notable subfields of Computer Science include:
- [[Artificial Intelligence]]: deals with the development of intelligent systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as [[Natural Language Processing]] and [[Robotics]].
- [[Computer Networks]]: focuses on the design, implementation, and management of [[Network Architecture]]s, including [[Internet Protocol]] and [[Network Security]].
- [[Database Systems]]: involves the design, implementation, and management of [[Database Management Systems]], including [[Data Modeling]] and [[Query Optimization]].

## Applications
Computer Science has numerous applications in various industries, including:
- [[Healthcare]]: [[Medical Imaging]], [[Electronic Health Records]], and [[Telemedicine]].
- [[Finance]]: [[Algorithmic Trading]], [[Risk Management]], and [[Cryptocurrency]].
- [[Education]]: [[Online Learning Platforms]], [[Intelligent Tutoring Systems]], and [[Educational Software]].

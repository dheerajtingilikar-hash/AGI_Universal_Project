# Cultural Anthropology
Cultural anthropology is a branch of [[Anthropology]] that focuses on the study of human cultures, behaviors, and societies.

## Overview
Cultural anthropology is a [[Social Science]] that examines the diversity of human cultures and experiences. It involves the study of [[Culture]], [[Society]], and [[Human Behavior]] in different parts of the world. Cultural anthropologists use various methods, including [[Ethnography]] and [[Participant Observation]], to gather data and understand the complexities of human cultures.

## Concept Map
The concept map of cultural anthropology includes:
* [[Cultural Relativism]]: the idea that cultures should be understood on their own terms
* [[Ethnocentrism]]: the tendency to view one's own culture as superior to others
* [[Symbolic Anthropology]]: the study of symbols and meanings in different cultures
* [[Structural Functionalism]]: the study of how social structures and institutions function in a society
* [[Postcolonial Theory]]: the study of the impact of colonialism on cultures and societies
* [[Globalization]]: the study of the global flow of people, goods, and ideas

## Key Terms
* [[Anthropologist]]: a person who studies human cultures and behaviors
* [[Fieldwork]]: the process of conducting research in a specific cultural context
* [[Cultural Competence]]: the ability to understand and appreciate different cultures
* [[Intercultural Communication]]: the study of how people from different cultures communicate with each other

## Related Fields
* [[Sociology]]: the study of human social behavior and societies
* [[Psychology]]: the study of human behavior and mental processes
* [[History]]: the study of past human cultures and societies
* [[Linguistics]]: the study of human language and communication

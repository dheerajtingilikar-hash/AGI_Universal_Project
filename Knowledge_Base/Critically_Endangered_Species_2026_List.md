# Critically Endangered Species 2026 List

## Overview

The Critically Endangered Species 2026 List is a compilation of species that are considered to be at high risk of extinction in the wild. This list is based on the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) Red List, which is widely recognized as the most authoritative assessment of the conservation status of species globally.

### Criteria for Inclusion

Species are considered Critically Endangered if they meet any of the following criteria:

- A1: The species is considered to be functionally extinct in the wild.
- A2: The species is considered to be at high risk of extinction in the wild due to a decline in population size or range.
- A3: The species is considered to be at high risk of extinction in the wild due to a decline in population size or range, and there is a high likelihood of extinction in the near future.

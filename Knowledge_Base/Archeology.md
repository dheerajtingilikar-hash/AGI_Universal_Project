# Archeology
## Overview
Archeology is the [[Science]] of studying [[History]] through the recovery, analysis, and interpretation of [[Artifact]]s and other material remains. It involves the systematic excavation and examination of [[Archaeological Site]]s to understand the [[Culture]]s, [[Technology]], and [[Environment]]s of past [[Society|societies]].

## Concept Map
The field of archeology is closely related to other disciplines, including:
* [[Anthropology]]
* [[History]]
* [[Geology]]
* [[Paleontology]]
It involves various techniques, such as:
* [[Excavation]]
* [[Radiocarbon Dating]]
* [[Stratigraphy]]
Archeological findings can be categorized into different types, including:
* [[Artifact]]
* [[Fossil]]
* [[Monument]]
* [[Inspection|Inscription]]

## Subfields
Archeology has several subfields, including:
* [[Classical Archaeology]]
* [[Egyptology]]
* [[Underwater Archaeology]]
* [[Bioarchaeology]]

## Methods
Archeologists use a range of methods to analyze and interpret data, including:
* [[Statistical Analysis]]
* [[Computer Simulation]]
* [[Remote Sensing]]
* [[Geographic Information System|GIS]]

## Applications
The knowledge and techniques of archeology have various applications, including:
* [[Cultural Resource Management]]
* [[Heritage Preservation]]
* [[Museum Studies]]
* [[Tourism]]

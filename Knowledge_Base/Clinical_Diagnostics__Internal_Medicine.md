# Clinical Diagnostics & Internal Medicine

## Overview

Clinical diagnostics and internal medicine are multidisciplinary fields that involve the application of medical science to diagnose and manage diseases affecting the internal organs and systems of the human body. The primary goal of clinical diagnostics is to identify the underlying cause of a patient's symptoms and develop an effective treatment plan.

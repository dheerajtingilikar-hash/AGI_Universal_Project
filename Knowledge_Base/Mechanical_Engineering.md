# Mechanical Engineering
Mechanical engineering is the discipline that applies [[Engineering]] principles to design, construct, and maintain [[Machine]]s.

## Overview
Mechanical engineering is a broad field that encompasses the design, development, and operation of [[Mechanical System]]s. It involves the application of [[Physics]] and [[Mathematics]] to analyze and solve problems related to [[Thermodynamics]], [[Mechanics]], and [[Materials Science]]. Mechanical engineers work on a wide range of projects, from [[Robotics]] and [[Aerospace Engineering]] to [[Automotive Engineering]] and [[Biomechanical Engineering]].

## Concept Map
The field of mechanical engineering can be broken down into several key areas, including:
* [[Thermodynamics]]: the study of [[Heat]] and [[Energy]] transfer
* [[Mechanics of Materials]]: the study of the properties and behavior of [[Material]]s under various types of [[Load]]
* [[Mechanical System]]s: the design and analysis of systems that involve [[Mechanism]]s, [[Machine]]s, and [[Control System]]s
* [[Manufacturing Engineering]]: the application of mechanical engineering principles to the design and operation of [[Manufacturing Process]]es
* [[Robotics]]: the design, construction, and operation of [[Robot]]s and [[Automation]] systems

Related fields include:
* [[Aerospace Engineering]]
* [[Automotive Engineering]]
* [[Biomechanical Engineering]]
* [[Civil Engineering]]
* [[Electrical Engineering]]
